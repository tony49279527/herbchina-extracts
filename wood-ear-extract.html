Wood Ear Extract

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Wood Ear Extract is a fruiting-body mushroom ingredient standardized to polysaccharides, a traditional black fungus material used in Asian wellness and food concepts. It fits capsules, functional foods, and cosmetics. As a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers, HerbChina Extracts helps overseas supplement, food, beverage, and cosmetics brands compare commonly requested market specifications. Buyers share their target spec and we confirm feasibility honestly before quotation.

FAQ

Common questions.

Which polysaccharide ranges, 20–50%, are commonly requested for Wood Ear Extract?
Commonly requested options include polysaccharides at 20–50%. Buyers can share target marker, carrier, particle size, and testing expectations; we confirm feasibility with vetted partner producers.
Can you supply Wood Ear Extract with polysaccharides tested by UV or HPLC to a specific percentage between 20 and 50%?
Wood Ear Extract is standardized on polysaccharides, with 20% to 50% commonly requested and tested by UV or HPLC depending on the agreed scope. COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed against your target specification. Once we receive your target spec, feasibility is confirmed through our partner producers.
For wood ear extract at 50% polysaccharides, how do you verify the polysaccharide content, and is the fruiting body the source material?
Wood ear polysaccharides are commonly requested between 20% and 50% by UV or HPLC, and the method is confirmed against your target specification. We check with partner producers that the starting material and plant part, typically the fruiting body, match what your specification calls for. COA and TDS for the confirmed grade and method are available on request.
