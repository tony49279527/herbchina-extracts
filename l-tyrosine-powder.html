L-Tyrosine Powder

L-Tyrosine — high-purity amino acid powder, commonly 99%+, for focus and cognitive-support formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

L-Tyrosine is a conditionally essential amino acid supplied as a free-flowing, high-purity powder for capsule, powder-blend and beverage formats. It is a standard input for brands building focus and cognitive-support lines, often alongside other amino acids and nootropics. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through vetted partner producers and confirm each buyer's assay, particle and documentation targets before quoting.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is the material fermentation-derived or synthetically produced?
L-Tyrosine is typically produced by fermentation or enzymatic conversion, and buyers often specify which route they require for label and documentation reasons. Tell us your preference and we will confirm honestly what our partner producers can supply.
What assay grades and particle forms are commonly requested?
Most inquiries target 99%+ by HPLC or titration, in either a standard powder or a finer-mesh grade for blending. Exact assay method and particle distribution are agreed per buyer specification.
How is the COA and TDS handled?
COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. Requirements vary by market, so the panel is set with you rather than assumed.
