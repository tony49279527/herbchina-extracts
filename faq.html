FAQ

Straight answers, before you ask.

The questions extract buyers ask us most — answered without fine print. Anything else, just ask us directly.

What are your minimum order quantities?
Minimum order quantities vary by ingredient and order volume. Share your target quantity when you inquire and we'll confirm our current terms.
What are your lead times?
Lead times depend on the ingredient and order size. Every quotation states the confirmed production and shipping schedule before you commit.
How are products packaged?
Packaging is matched to the product and the order volume. Confirm the current packaging options for your ingredient when you inquire.
What quality documentation do you provide?
Every order is documented against your specification — active markers, contaminant screening, and microbial results. Certificates of analysis (COA), technical data sheets (TDS), and company qualification documents are available on request.
Can you match a custom specification?
Send your target extract ratio, active-compound level, mesh size, and packaging requirements. We'll tell you honestly what we can match before any commitment.
Do you provide samples?
Talk to us about what you need to evaluate. We'll confirm what's possible for your ingredient and volume.
Which markets do you serve?
We're based in Xi'an, China and work with overseas buyers. Share your destination market when you inquire and we'll confirm documentation for your import requirements.
How do I start an inquiry?
Use the inquiry form on our contact page, call us, or reach us on WeChat at +86 135 7292 7148. We respond within 24 hours.

Still have questions?

Send your spec sheet or just your questions — we'll answer directly.
