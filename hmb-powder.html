HMB Powder

Bulk calcium HMB powder for sports nutrition formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

HMB powder is supplied as calcium beta-hydroxy beta-methylbutyrate, a metabolite of leucine widely used in sports nutrition and recovery-positioned formulations. It appears in capsule, powder and ready-to-mix formats. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source Ca-HMB through partner producers and confirm grade, form and documentation feasibility after receiving your target specification.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is your HMB supplied as free acid or calcium salt, and which form do buyers usually request?
Calcium HMB (Ca-HMB) is the most commonly requested form for supplement applications, though free acid HMB is also traded. We confirm the available form against your target spec on inquiry.
What Ca-HMB content grades are commonly requested for sports nutrition products?
Buyers typically request Ca-HMB grades in the range commonly traded for sports nutrition, with exact content confirmed on inquiry. We do not present a fixed grade as inventory without your spec.

COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. We review required parameters with the partner producer before quoting.
