Water Chestnut Extract

Water chestnut — corm-derived food ingredient material for starch-based food formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Water chestnut extract is produced from the corm of Eleocharis dulcis, a starchy aquatic vegetable used widely in Asian food products. The material is typically requested as a food-ingredient powder rather than a standardized botanical marker extract, making it relevant to bakery, snack and specialty starch formulations. As a Xi'an trading company we source through partner producers and confirm feasibility on receipt of your target spec.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is water chestnut extract a standardized extract or a food-ingredient powder?
Water chestnut corm material is generally traded as a food-ingredient powder rather than a marker-standardized botanical extract, since its value sits in the starch and native corm composition. Tell us how you intend to use it and we will confirm which powder grade from our partner producers fits your application.
What production method is typically used for water chestnut powder?
Water chestnut corms are usually cleaned, processed and dried before milling into powder; some grades are further treated for solubility or particle size depending on the end application. Actual production route is confirmed against your target spec, and a COA is available on request.
Can you match a specific particle size or starch content for water chestnut powder?
We operate as a trading company sourcing through partner producers, so custom mesh, starch or moisture targets are assessed per inquiry. Send your target specification and intended application, and we will confirm feasibility before quoting.
