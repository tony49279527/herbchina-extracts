Turmeric Extract

Curcuma longa — Rhizome-derived Curcuma longa extract standardized to curcuminoids — for supplement, functional food, and beverage brands formulating inflammation-positioned and color-stable products.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Turmeric extract is among the highest-volume botanical ingredients globally, standardized to curcuminoids for supplement and functional food applications. Buyers source it for capsules, gummies, beverages, and powder blends where a documented curcuminoid level is required. As a trading company, we work with partner producers and confirm supply against your target specification — we don't present confirmed stock specs or hold certifications we can't document.

Specifications below reflect commonly requested options in the market — not a stock list. Share your target spec and we'll confirm exactly what we can supply, honestly.

FAQ

Common questions.

What curcuminoid spec options are commonly requested?
The most common market request is 95% curcuminoids by HPLC. Turmeric powder ratios are also requested for food and beverage applications. Share your target and we'll confirm what can be supplied.
Can you supply custom specifications?
Yes — we work against your target specification for marker content, mesh size, appearance, and testing scope. Send your spec sheet and we'll confirm feasibility with partner producers.
What documentation is available?
COA and TDS can be arranged on request. Testing scope is agreed against your specification — we don't claim in-house lab testing. State your document requirements in the inquiry.
