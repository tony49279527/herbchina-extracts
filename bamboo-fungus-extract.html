Bamboo Fungus Extract

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Bamboo fungus extract is a mushroom powder produced from the fruiting body of Dictyophora indusiata, the veiled lady mushroom, standardized to polysaccharides. Its dual gourmet and beauty positioning keeps it in demand across supplement and skin-nutrition concepts. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through vetted partner producers and give buyers a candid read on what a target specification can realistically deliver.

FAQ

Common questions.

What part of the bamboo fungus is extracted, and is the veil included?
Traded material is generally fruiting body, with the characteristic net-like veil sometimes removed before processing. If your specification distinguishes between whole fruiting body and partial material, raise it up front so it can be addressed in the agreed testing scope.
How are polysaccharides and beta-glucan evaluated for this species?
Polysaccharides at 20-50% by UV are commonly requested, with beta-glucan frequently reported separately using a glucan-specific method because total polysaccharide figures can include non-glucan material. The exact panel is agreed per buyer specification.
Can COA and TDS be arranged against our target polysaccharide level?
COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. Send your target polysaccharide or beta-glucan figure and intended format and we will confirm feasibility with a partner producer.
