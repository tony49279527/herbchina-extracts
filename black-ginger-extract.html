FAQ

Common questions.

How are polymethoxyflavones in black ginger extract typically determined?
Polymethoxyflavones such as 5,7-dimethoxyflavone are commonly quantified by HPLC, with the sum of methoxyflavones sometimes reported as a group. Testing scope is confirmed against your target spec, and a COA is available on request.
Which part of Kaempferia parviflora is used, and does rhizome age matter?
Black ginger extract is sourced from the rhizome of Kaempferia parviflora. Rhizome maturity and origin can influence polymethoxyflavone profiles, so if your target spec calls for a particular rhizome grade or origin we will confirm sourcing feasibility with our partner producers.
Can you supply black ginger extract matched to a specific polymethoxyflavone percentage?
As a trading company sourcing through partner producers, we assess custom polymethoxyflavone specifications per inquiry. Send your target percentage, preferred HPLC method and intended application, and we will confirm feasibility before quoting.
