Chromium Picolinate Powder

Chromium Picolinate — a chromium complex positioned for metabolic and glucose-management formulation concepts.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Chromium Picolinate Powder is a coordination complex of trivalent chromium and picolinic acid, widely used in the supplement industry. It is commonly traded at chromium content of 12% or higher by assay, appearing as a fine reddish-purple powder. Formulators use it in capsules and tablets positioned around metabolic and glucose-management concepts. HerbChina Extracts is a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers; buyers share a target spec and we confirm feasibility honestly.

FAQ

Common questions.

How is chromium content in Chromium Picolinate Powder typically assayed?
Chromium content is commonly assayed by titration or ICP methods, with 12%+ being the most frequently requested level. Buyers should state the assay range and test method in their target spec; COA/TDS can be arranged on request and testing scope is agreed per buyer specification.
What is the typical physical form and color of your chromium picolinate material?
Chromium picolinate is typically a fine, reddish-purple crystalline powder. Color and particle size should be confirmed against your spec, as these can vary between production batches and suppliers.
What is the process for confirming a custom chromium picolinate specification?
