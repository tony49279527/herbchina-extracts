Hawthorn Berry Extract

Crataegus pinnatifida / monogyna — berry extract standardized to vitexin and flavonoids, positioned around heart and circulation formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Hawthorn berry is a long-standing botanical in heart-health and circulation supplement categories. Extract is standardized to vitexin or total flavonoids, with 1-2% vitexin and higher flavonoid levels commonly requested. We source through vetted partner producers and confirm each buyer's marker and carrier requirements before quoting.

Specifications below reflect commonly requested options in the market — not a stock list. Share your target spec and we'll confirm exactly what we can supply, honestly.
