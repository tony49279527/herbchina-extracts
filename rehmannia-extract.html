FAQ

Common questions.

Is the extract made from raw root or prepared/processed root, and does the spec differ?
Both appear in trade, and they are not the same starting material — processing changes color, sugar content and the marker profile, with catalpol in particular shifting after traditional preparation steps. If your formulation specifies one form, say so clearly, because substituting the other changes the finished product. We will confirm which the partner producer can supply.
How is catalpol quantified and what method should we ask for?
Catalpol is normally quantified by HPLC against a reference standard, and some producers also report total iridoid glycosides. Method and reference standard choice affect the number, so specify the method your QC will run. The testing scope is agreed per buyer specification and documentation can be arranged on request.
Why is the powder so dark and hygroscopic, and can that be managed in a capsule line?
Rehmannia extract is naturally dark and tends to pick up moisture because of its sugar and polysaccharide content, which can cause sticking on capsule and tablet lines. Lower-ratio or carrier-supported grades often flow better. If your line is sensitive to hygroscopicity or color, describe the constraint and we will confirm what grades can be held.
