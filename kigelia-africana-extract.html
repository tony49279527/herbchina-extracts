Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Kigelia Africana Extract is a botanical powder derived from the fruit of Kigelia africana, a tree widely distributed across sub-Saharan Africa. Cosmetic formulators position it around firmness and skin-conditioning concepts in creams, serums and masks. Demand has grown alongside the clean-beauty and African botanicals trend. HerbChina Extracts is a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers; buyers share a target spec and we confirm feasibility honestly before any commitment.

FAQ

Common questions.

Which plant part is your Kigelia africana material sourced from?
The material is sourced from the fruit of Kigelia africana, which is the part most commonly traded for cosmetic use. Buyers should specify fruit-derived material in their target spec, since leaf or bark material is sometimes seen in the market and shows a different profile.
How is Kigelia Africana Extract typically standardized?
Market grades are commonly standardized to flavonoid or iridoid content, or offered as ratio extracts such as 4:1 to 10:1. The exact marker and test method should be agreed per buyer specification, and COA/TDS can be arranged on request.
What is the process for confirming a custom Kigelia specification?
Share your target marker, assay range, test method and intended cosmetic application. As a trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers, we check feasibility against that spec and confirm honestly what can be matched.
