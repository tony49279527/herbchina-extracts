FAQ

Common questions.

Should porcini extract be made from mycelium or fruiting body, and how do buyers tell the difference?
Porcini extract is traditionally made from the Boletus edulis fruiting body, and buyers often specify fruiting body explicitly on the label. Mycelium-based material is also traded in the mushroom category generally, so if fruiting body only is required, state it clearly and we will confirm the producer's starting material.
Which grades and markers are commonly requested for porcini extract?
Most buyers ask for 4:1 to 10:1 fruiting-body extracts, chosen for umami intensity and color. Where a nutritional panel claim is planned, beta-glucan or polysaccharide standardized grades are also requested. The right choice depends on whether the priority is flavor, label claim, or both.
How do you handle flavor and organoleptic requirements, and can documentation be arranged?
Share your target ratio or marker, intended application, and any aroma, color or solubility expectations, and we will check feasibility with vetted partner producers honestly. A COA can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification.
