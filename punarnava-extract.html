Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Punarnava extract is produced from the root of Boerhavia diffusa and is a recognized ingredient in Ayurvedic-inspired supplement development. Buyers frequently request grades standardized to boeravinones, a group of rotenoid-type compounds used as the analytical marker for this botanical. We are a trading company operating from Xi'an, sourcing through partner producers and confirming feasibility only once a buyer's target specification has been received.

FAQ

Common questions.

Which boeravinone is used as the marker, and how is it analyzed?
Boeravinones are a family of related rotenoid compounds, and specifications may reference a single boeravinone or a total figure. HPLC is the standard analytical approach, though the exact reference standard should be agreed between buyer and supplier. Because the marker family is broad, we confirm the feasible specification range only after seeing your target.
Is punarnava extract always made from root material?
Root is the conventional starting material for punarnava extract, but some supply chains include aerial parts, which can dilute the boeravinone profile. If root-only sourcing is required by your specification, flag it in your inquiry so partner producers can be checked against that requirement.
How do I obtain a COA or TDS for a punarnava grade?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. Send your marker, method and acceptance criteria and we will align documentation accordingly.
