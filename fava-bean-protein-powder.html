Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Fava bean protein powder is produced from the seed of Vicia faba and has moved quickly into the mainstream of plant-protein sourcing, driven by the wider bioactive peptide trend in sports nutrition. Buyers commonly request protein levels between 60% and 90%, with additional interest in peptide fractions and declared amino acid profiles. As a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers, we review your required protein content, peptide specification and format, and confirm feasibility without overstating what a given producer can deliver.

FAQ

Common questions.

What protein content grades are commonly requested, and on what basis are they expressed?
Protein levels from 60% to 90% are among the most commonly requested options, usually expressed on a dry or as-is basis with the calculation method declared. Because the basis affects the number materially, we confirm both the target percentage and the analytical basis with the producer before quoting.
How is fava bean protein produced, and does the process affect peptide content?
Fava bean protein is generally obtained by wet or dry fractionation of the seed, with enzymatic hydrolysis used where a peptide fraction is requested. The chosen route influences molecular weight distribution and functional behaviour in the finished product, so we align the requested process with the partner producer before confirming feasibility.
Can COA and TDS be arranged for fava bean protein powder?
A COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with the testing scope agreed per buyer specification. Provide your target protein level, basis and any peptide or amino acid parameters, and we will confirm what the producing partner can document.
