Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Wild yam extract comes from the root of Dioscorea villosa and is sought by supplement developers for its diosgenin content. Demand concentrates in women's wellness positioning, where formulators request defined diosgenin grades for capsules, tablets and specialty blends. Operating as a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through vetted partner producers and verify what is realistic against your target specification.

FAQ

Common questions.

What diosgenin grades are typically requested for wild yam extract?
Diosgenin content grades are the most commonly requested specification in this category, though achievable levels depend on the source root and the concentration process. We review your target diosgenin figure and preferred analytical method before confirming what a partner producer can realistically deliver.
How can buyers approach adulteration checks on wild yam root material?
Because wild yam is traded alongside other Dioscorea species, buyers often request species confirmation and diosgenin assay as checkpoints. The testing scope is agreed per buyer specification, and documentation can be arranged on request through our partner producers.
Which plant part is used for wild yam extract?
The extract is produced from the root of Dioscorea villosa. Root quality and harvest timing influence diosgenin content, so buyers usually define both the marker range and the plant part in their purchase specification before sourcing.
