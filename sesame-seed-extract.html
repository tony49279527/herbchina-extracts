Sesame Seed Extract

Sesame seed — Sesamum indicum extract for nutrition and cosmetic formulation work.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Sesame seed extract is derived from the seed of Sesamum indicum, valued for lignans such as sesamin and sesamolin. It appears in nutrition-positioned supplement concepts and in cosmetic and personal-care formulations, often on lignan-standardized grades. As a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through partner producers, we confirm marker grade, format and documentation once your target specification is received.

Specifications below reflect commonly requested options in the market — not a stock list. Share your target spec and we'll confirm exactly what we can supply, honestly.

FAQ

Common questions.

What sesamin and sesamolin grades of sesame seed extract are commonly traded?
Buyers request a range of lignan-standardized grades, from around 10% up to higher sesamin concentrates, depending on whether the application is nutritional or cosmetic. These are market reference ranges only; actual specification and method are confirmed on inquiry against your target spec.
How are sesamin and sesamolin content in sesame seed extract tested?
Sesamin and sesamolin are typically assessed by HPLC where individual lignan figures are required. We confirm the applicable method, marker basis and limits with partner producers based on your target specification.
What documents can be supplied with sesame seed extract?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. Please share your required lignan method, limits and any allergen-related documentation needs so alignment can be checked before quoting.
