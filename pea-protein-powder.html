Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Pea protein powder is extracted from the seed of Pisum sativum and is one of the most widely used plant proteins in sports nutrition and plant-based food. Buyers most often request isolate grades in the 80-85% protein range, and some evaluate textured or concentrate options for different applications. Demand continues to grow with the plant-protein category across powders, bars and beverages. We source through vetted partner producers and confirm feasibility against your target spec.

FAQ

Common questions.

What protein content grades are typically traded for pea protein?
The most frequently requested specification is pea protein isolate in the 80-85% protein range on a dry basis, measured by Kjeldahl or combustion nitrogen analysis with a conversion factor. Concentrate grades at lower protein levels and textured pea protein are also available for specific applications. Which grade suits you depends on your target protein claim and the processing the matrix can tolerate.
How is pea protein produced, and does the process affect flavor or solubility?
Pea protein is typically produced by wet fractionation, in which protein is separated from starch and fiber and then dried, or by dry fractionation for some concentrate grades. Wet-processed isolates generally offer higher protein purity and better dispersibility but can carry more of the characteristic pea flavor, which formulators often mask. If flavor is a constraint, mention it so the closest suitable grade can be evaluated.
What documentation is provided with a pea protein sample or quotation?
COA and TDS can be arranged on request; testing scope is agreed per buyer specification. Provide your target protein percentage, method, microbiological limits and allergen-related requirements, and we will confirm what the partner producer can supply.
