Cinnamon Bark Extract

Cinnamomum cassia / verum — bark extract standardized to cinnamaldehyde and polyphenols, positioned around metabolic formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Cinnamon bark extract is a dual-purpose botanical traded in metabolic wellness supplements and as a natural flavor system. Standardization to cinnamaldehyde or total polyphenols is common, with 8-10% polyphenols and higher cinnamaldehyde levels frequently requested. We source through vetted partner producers and confirm each buyer's marker before quoting.

Specifications below reflect commonly requested options in the market — not a stock list. Share your target spec and we'll confirm exactly what we can supply, honestly.
