Evening Primrose Extract

Evening primrose — GLA botanical for women's wellness formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Evening primrose extract is derived from the seeds of Oenothera biennis and is valued by formulators for its gamma-linolenic acid content. Buyers in the supplement space frequently request oil-content grades for softgel and capsule applications positioned around women's wellness. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through vetted partner producers and confirm feasibility against your target specification before proceeding.

FAQ

Common questions.

What GLA content can be supplied in evening primrose extract?
GLA oil-content grades are among the most commonly requested options in the market, and typical ranges vary by producer. Because oil-content specifications depend on the extraction and concentration route, we confirm the achievable range only after reviewing your target value and preferred test method.
Which part of the plant is used and how is the extract produced?
The material is sourced from Oenothera biennis seeds. Producers typically work via cold-press or solvent-based oil extraction followed by concentration, and the exact route affects GLA retention and carrier format. We can discuss which production approach suits your formulation once your specification is shared.
Can documentation be provided for evening primrose extract?
COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with the testing scope agreed per buyer specification. We coordinate documentation through our vetted partner producers so the parameters relevant to your application are covered.
