FAQ

Common questions.

How is protein content determined in chickpea powder, and what grade is typical?
Protein is normally assessed by Kjeldahl or Dumas combustion, with fiber measured by enzymatic-gravimetric methods. Protein 20% is a commonly requested grade, though the exact specification is confirmed on inquiry against your target.
Is your chickpea powder made from whole seed or dehulled flour, and how fine is the particle size?
Material is milled from Cicer arietinum seed flour, and producers may offer whole or dehulled variants. Particle size varies by grade; buyers should confirm mesh and flour type against their application, with COA and TDS available on request.
Can you supply chickpea powder tested for fiber or protein against our own spec?
Yes. Provide your target protein or fiber range and intended use, and we will confirm feasibility through our partner producers. Testing scope is agreed against your spec, and COA/TDS are available on request.
