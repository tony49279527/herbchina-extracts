Barley Grass Powder

Barley grass — young-leaf green powder for greens and wellness-blend formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Barley grass powder comes from the young leaf of Hordeum vulgare, harvested before the plant matures and processed into either juice powder or whole-leaf powder. Buyers often specify chlorophyll content and, for juice grades, SOD activity as a distinguishing marker for premium wellness positioning. It is a staple of the greens-powder category and appears in capsule, sachet and bulk blend formats. We source through vetted partner producers and confirm feasibility against your target spec.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is SOD activity a standard barley grass specification, and how is it measured?
SOD activity is not universal across all barley grass grades but is frequently requested for juice powders positioned as premium greens ingredients. It is typically expressed in units per gram and measured by enzyme activity assay, which is sensitive to heat history and storage. If SOD is central to your claim, specify the assay method and minimum activity so the producer can confirm what is achievable.
What distinguishes barley grass juice powder from whole-leaf powder in formulation?
Juice powder is made by pressing fresh young leaf and drying the juice, producing a more concentrated, brighter green ingredient with lower fiber. Whole-leaf powder is milled dried leaf and carries the full fiber fraction, which affects taste, texture and dispersal in drink mixes. Many buyers stock both and choose by dosage form; tell us which grade your formulation references.
Which technical documents accompany a barley grass offer?
COA and TDS can be arranged on request; testing scope is agreed per buyer specification. Share your target chlorophyll or SOD figure, grade type and contaminant limits, and we will confirm what the partner producer can realistically provide.
