Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

It appears in traditional digestive-positioned supplement concepts and in broader Ayurvedic product lines. FAQ

Common questions.

How are cyperene and sesquiterpene grades usually expressed and tested?
Buyers commonly reference cyperene or total sesquiterpene percentages, with GC-based methods often cited for volatile marker evaluation. Since market grades differ, please specify your target marker level and analytical method for a feasibility review.
What part of Cyperus rotundus is used, and is the rhizome processed fresh or dried?
The rhizome is the standard starting material, normally dried before extraction and standardization. If your specification references a particular extract ratio or requires a defined solvent system, note that in your inquiry so it can be assessed.
What is the process for confirming a custom specification?
Send your target spec, including marker range and test methods, and we will review feasibility with our partner producers. COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec.
