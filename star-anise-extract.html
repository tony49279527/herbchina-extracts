Star Anise Extract

Star anise — Illicium verum fruit extract for flavor and confectionery development.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Star anise extract is derived from the fruit of Illicium verum, a key flavor material characterized by its anethole content and used widely in confectionery, beverage and savory applications. Food and flavor buyers request it on anethole-standardized grades or as concentrated flavor inputs. As a Xi'an trading company sourcing through partner producers, we confirm grade, format and documentation after receiving your target specification.

FAQ

Common questions.

What anethole grades of star anise extract are commonly requested?
Buyers frequently request anethole-standardized grades in the 80% to 90% range, and some projects reference volatile oil instead. These are market reference ranges only; actual specification, format and test method are confirmed on inquiry against your target spec.
How is star anise extract distinguished from other anise-flavored sources?
Star anise (Illicium verum) is distinct from anise seed and fennel, and buyers sometimes request source confirmation and marker testing to verify the declared botanical. We confirm the applicable identity and anethole test scope with partner producers based on your specification.
What documentation is available for star anise extract?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. Please share your anethole method, limits and any additional parameters so documents can be aligned before quoting.
