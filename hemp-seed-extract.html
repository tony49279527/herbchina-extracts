Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Hemp seed extract is produced from Cannabis sativa seed, a non-controlled food-grade material supplied for its protein and omega-3 content. Buyers request it for protein blends, functional foods and nutrition powder concepts; it is distinct from CBD ingredients and no such claims apply. Because seed-derived material is food-grade, specifications tend to focus on protein content and fatty acid profile. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through vetted partner producers and confirm feasibility per buyer specification.

FAQ

Common questions.

How is protein content typically reported for hemp seed material?
Protein is usually reported on a dry-weight basis using a nitrogen-derived method, and buyers often specify a target percentage range alongside a desired omega-3 to omega-6 ratio. Different calculation conventions can shift reported figures, so the basis should be agreed in writing. We match your stated targets against partner producer capability.
Is this material derived only from seed, and is it free of controlled cannabinoid material?
Yes, the material referenced here is Cannabis sativa seed, which is a non-controlled food-grade input, and it is not a CBD or cannabinoid extract. Seed sourcing means cannabinoid content is not a relevant specification dimension, but buyers often request confirmation of the plant part used. That verification can be included in your agreed testing scope.
What documentation can be prepared for a hemp seed extract order?
A COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification, typically covering protein, fat and fatty acid parameters. Share the figures you need documented and we will confirm what the producer can support before you commit to an order.
