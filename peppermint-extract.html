FAQ

Common questions.

What menthol levels are typically requested, and how is menthol measured?
Menthol content is the primary marker, often reported by GC, and buyers sometimes also request menthone or total volatile oil figures. Typical requested ranges vary considerably between a low-menthol standardized powder and a high-menthol extract, so the percentage and method should be fixed in your specification. We check your target against what partner producers can offer.
Is the extract made from leaf only, and could other mint species be substituted?
Mentha piperita leaf is the source material referenced for true peppermint extract. Other Mentha species, such as spearmint, carry different marker profiles, so species identity is a genuine adulteration checkpoint. We recommend agreeing species confirmation, whether by botanical or analytical means, within your specification.
Can COA and TDS documents be prepared for our target menthol specification?
A COA and TDS can be arranged on request, and the testing scope is agreed per buyer specification so it reflects your parameters. Send your target menthol level, method preference and any limits, and we will confirm which documents the producer can support ahead of ordering.
