FAQ

Common questions.

What anthocyanin level is commonly requested?
25% anthocyanins is the most commonly requested specification in the market, though other standardized levels are also traded. Tell us your target and we will confirm feasibility.
What are typical adulteration checkpoints for bilberry extract, and can the COA cover them?
Bilberry anthocyanin extracts are sometimes adulterated with anthocyanins from other berries such as black rice, blackcurrant, or grape skin, so buyers often request authenticity markers alongside the stated 25% anthocyanosides by UV/HPLC. COA and TDS can be arranged on request, and we confirm testing scope against your target specification, including any identity or adulteration checks you require. Feasibility of specific test methods is confirmed through partner producers after receiving your target spec.
Is a water-soluble grade available for beverages?
Various carriers and grades are commonly traded for beverage use. Advise your solubility requirement and we will respond honestly on what partner producers can supply.
