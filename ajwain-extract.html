Ajwain Extract

Ajwain — aromatic seed botanical for digestive-positioned formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Ajwain extract is a botanical powder derived from Trachyspermum ammi seed, standardized around thymol, the volatile marker that drives most buyer interest. Demand comes mainly from supplement and traditional-formulation brands building digestive-positioned lines. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through partner producers and confirm feasibility against each buyer's target spec, so grades and documentation are matched before any offer.

FAQ

Common questions.

How is thymol content typically assayed in ajwain seed extract, and which grades do buyers request?
Thymol is usually determined by GC or GC-MS on the volatile fraction; buyers often request standardized thymol grades, with the exact percentage confirmed against the buyer's target spec. Common requests we see are stated as thymol content bands, and we confirm the feasible range with partner producers after receiving your spec.
What plant part and source material is used for your ajwain extract offers?
Offers are based on Trachyspermum ammi seed, the aromatic seed material typically used for thymol-standardized extracts. Source material origin and harvest lots differ between partner producers, so origin details are confirmed on inquiry once your target spec is received.
Can you provide a COA and TDS for a specific ajwain thymol grade?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. Because we are a trading company sourcing through partner producers, we confirm the applicable documentation with the producer before quoting.
