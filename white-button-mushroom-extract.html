FAQ

Common questions.

Is beta-glucan measured across total glucans or only the 1,3/1,6 linked fraction?
Beta-glucan specifications can be reported as total glucan or differentiated by linkage type, and methods differ accordingly. Share the fraction and method you need, and we will confirm what can be tested against your spec.
Is the extract made from fruiting body only, or can mycelium be present?
This material is derived from Agaricus bisporus fruiting body as standard. If you require a declaration confirming fruiting-body-only sourcing, raise this in your inquiry for verification.
Can the ergothioneine content be adjusted to a specific grade for our immune formulation?
Ergothioneine grades are commonly requested at varying levels, but actual specs are confirmed on inquiry. Provide your target ergothioneine or beta-glucan range and we will follow up with documentation availability.
