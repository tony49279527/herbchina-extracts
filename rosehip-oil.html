Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

It is widely used in skincare and cosmetic formulations targeting facial and body care. FAQ

Common questions.

Is your rosehip oil cold-pressed from seed, and does it contain vitamin A naturally?
The oil is cold-pressed from Rosa canina seed and naturally contains trans-retinoic acid precursors often reported as vitamin A. Levels vary by batch, so buyers should confirm the analytical basis; COA and TDS are available on request.
How is linoleic acid content measured in rosehip oil?
Linoleic acid is typically quantified by gas chromatography as part of the fatty acid profile, with vitamin A assessed separately. The exact marker and method are confirmed against your target spec before quotation.
Can you provide rosehip oil tested to a specified linoleic acid or vitamin A range?
Provide your target linoleic acid or vitamin A range and intended cosmetic application, and we will confirm feasibility through our partner producers. Listed grades are commonly requested specifications, not inventory commitments; documentation is available on request.
