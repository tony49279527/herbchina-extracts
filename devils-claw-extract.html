FAQ

Common questions.

What harpagoside content is commonly requested in devil's claw extract?
Market grades are usually quoted on harpagoside percentage by HPLC, with standardized and ratio-based formats both traded. Because root lots vary, the achievable harpagoside level depends on the raw material. We confirm the realistic range against your target specification before quoting.
Is the root sourced from wild or cultivated plants?
Harpagophytum procumbens is native to southern Africa, and both wild-collected and cultivated material circulate in the trade. Buyers with sourcing or sustainability requirements should note them, and we will check what the partner producer can document.
How is harpagoside verified for devil's claw extract?
COA and TDS can be arranged on request; testing scope is agreed per buyer specification. Tell us the marker, method and acceptance criteria you require, and we will confirm what the partner producer can support.
