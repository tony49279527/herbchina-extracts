Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Biotin Powder is D-biotin, a water-soluble B-complex vitamin widely used across the supplement and beauty-from-within categories. It is commonly traded at 99%+ purity, appearing as a white crystalline powder. Formulators use it in capsules, gummies and tablets positioned around hair, skin and nail concepts. Demand has grown alongside the beauty-from-within trend. FAQ

Common questions.

What grades of Biotin Powder are commonly traded?
Pure D-biotin is commonly traded at 99%+ by HPLC. Lower-concentration triturations such as 1% and 2% are also seen in the market for gummy and beverage applications, where precise low-dose handling matters.
Is your biotin supplied as D-biotin or a racemic mixture?
The material is supplied as D-biotin, the biologically active form. Buyers should specify D-biotin and its assay in their target spec, since material labeled simply as biotin can vary in isomeric composition.
How do I arrange COA and TDS for a biotin order?
COA/TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. Share your target assay, particle size and intended application, and as a trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers we check feasibility and confirm honestly.
