FAQ

Common questions.

What does cracked cell wall mean in practice, and how is it confirmed?
Cracked cell wall refers to mechanical disruption of the algal cell wall to improve nutrient accessibility, and buyers often specify a degree of cracking rather than a simple yes/no. Confirmation is typically by microscopy or a supplier-declared processing statement. We verify what the partner producer can actually document before quoting.
How are protein and chlorophyll measured, and what contaminant checks are typical?
Protein is commonly determined by Kjeldahl or a comparable nitrogen method, and chlorophyll by spectrophotometric means; buyers sometimes add a heavy metals panel and microbiological testing. Adulteration checkpoints include undeclared dilution with filler and mismatched protein results. The exact panel is agreed per buyer specification.
Can you arrange a COA/TDS for my grade requirements?
A COA/TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. Tell us your target protein level, cell-wall requirement and required test panel, and we will confirm feasibility honestly through our vetted partner producers.
