Eucommia — a chlorogenic-acid-standardized bark extract for joint-comfort and bone-health formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Eucommia extract is produced from the bark of Eucommia ulmoides, a tree native to China and long used in traditional botanical products. Buyers commonly request chlorogenic acid grades from 5% to 25% for joint-comfort and bone-health oriented formulations. We operate as a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers and confirm each target specification before quoting.

FAQ

Common questions.

Which chlorogenic acid grades are commonly traded, and is the assay by HPLC or UV?
Grades around 5%, 10%, 15% and 25% chlorogenic acid are commonly requested, with both HPLC and UV methods used in the market. Because the two methods can give different readings, we ask buyers to state the method in their spec and confirm feasibility accordingly.
Is the material bark-only, and how is bark sourced given Eucommia's slow growth?
The extract is made from Eucommia ulmoides bark. Because the tree is slow-growing, buyers sometimes ask about leaf-based alternatives; if that matters to your formulation, note it in your spec and we will confirm what vetted partner producers can supply and document.
How do we set up documentation for a specific chlorogenic acid specification?
Send your target grade, assay method and any additional parameters, and we will confirm feasibility with partner producers. A COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with the testing scope agreed per buyer specification.
