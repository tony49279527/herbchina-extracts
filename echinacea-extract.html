Echinacea Extract

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Echinacea purpurea extract is a long-established immune-positioning botanical, standardised to markers such as echinacoside and cichoric acid. Demand remains steady across North American and European supplement brands, particularly in seasonal immune blends. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through vetted partner producers and match buyers to realistic, widely-traded specifications rather than holding fixed stock.

Specifications below reflect commonly requested options in the market — not a stock list. Share your target spec and we'll confirm exactly what we can supply, honestly.

FAQ

Common questions.

Which plant part is your Echinacea extract sourced from?
Both aerial parts and root are commonly traded. Aerial parts are typical for echinacoside-led specs, while root is often requested for cichoric acid or dual-marker specifications. Buyers share the target part with us and we confirm feasibility through our partner producers.
Can you supply both echinacoside and cichoric acid standardised specs?
Yes. Single-marker and dual-marker specifications are both widely available in the market. Share your preferred marker levels and testing method and we will confirm what can be arranged.
How is documentation handled?
A COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. We do not publish fixed testing capabilities; rather, the analytical package is defined together with the buyer.
