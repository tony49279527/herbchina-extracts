Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Date extract is produced from the fruit of Phoenix dactylifera and is commonly standardised around polyphenol content or dietary fiber. Formulators in the functional-food and beverage space have shown growing interest in date-derived actives, particularly for Middle Eastern and natural-sweetener-style concepts. FAQ

Common questions.

How are polyphenols in date extract typically quantified?
Total polyphenol content is commonly measured by the Folin-Ciocalteu colorimetric method and expressed as gallic acid equivalents on a dried basis. Buyers should note that natural sugar content in date fruit can influence handling and flowability. We align the testing method and target value with your specification before sourcing.
Do you offer both polyphenol-standardised and fiber-standardised date extract grades?
Both marker routes are commonly requested in the market: polyphenol-standardised grades and fiber-standardised grades. They differ in extraction and drying approach, which affects taste and solubility. These are reference market grades only; specific parameters are confirmed on inquiry against your formulation needs.
Can you confirm COA and TDS against our date extract specification?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. As a trading company we work through partner producers, so we review your parameters first and confirm feasibility before documentation is issued.
