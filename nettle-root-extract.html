Nettle Root Extract

Urtica dioica — root extract standardized to sterols and lignans, positioned around men's health and prostate-wellness formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Nettle root is a well-established men's health botanical, frequently formulated alongside saw palmetto in prostate-wellness categories. Extract is standardized to sterols or lignans, with ratios such as 10:1 also commonly traded. We source through vetted partner producers and confirm each buyer's marker and carrier requirements before quoting.
