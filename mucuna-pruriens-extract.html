FAQ

Common questions.

How is L-DOPA measured, and does the extraction solvent matter for the spec?
L-DOPA content is commonly determined by HPLC. Buyers sometimes specify water or aqueous-alcohol extraction depending on their clean-label position, and the solvent system can influence the accompanying profile. We confirm what approach the partner producer uses before quoting so the spec matches what is actually achievable.
What L-DOPA grades are typically requested, and how do buyers check for adulteration?
Commonly requested options in the market sit around 15% and 20% L-DOPA, with lower-strength extract-ratio grades also traded. Adulteration checkpoints buyers raise include spiking with synthetic L-DOPA, unusual enantiomer ratios and undeclared carrier content; the specific checks are agreed per buyer specification.
What do you need from me to confirm suitability for my formulation?
Share your target L-DOPA level, seed origin preference, extraction approach and required test panel. We source through vetted partner producers and will confirm feasibility honestly; a COA/TDS can be arranged on request with testing scope agreed per buyer specification.
