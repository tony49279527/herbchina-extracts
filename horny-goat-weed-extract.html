Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Horny Goat Weed extract is a long-standing men's vitality botanical, standardised to icariin and sourced from Epimedium sagittatum leaf. It features in vitality, sports and traditional Chinese medicine-inspired formulations. Icariin 10–20% is the most commonly traded range. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through vetted partner producers and confirm each buyer's target specification honestly.
