Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Buyers most commonly specify grades around anethole content, with ratio extracts also requested. Demand tracks functional beverage and herbal tea development. FAQ

Common questions.

What anethole grades are commonly requested for fennel seed extract?
The market most often references anethole content, sometimes expressed as trans-anethole, with GC-based methods typically cited for volatile marker measurement. Grades range widely and are not standardized across suppliers, so the exact percentage, isomer basis and test method should be fixed in your specification. We check your stated grade against what our partner producers can support.
Is the extract produced from seed only, and how does extraction method affect the specification?
Foeniculum vulgare seed is the primary source material, though buyers occasionally reference other plant parts and those are treated as separate lines. Extraction route, whether solvent-based or steam-distillation related, influences volatile retention and marker recovery, so the production method should be agreed alongside the specification. Tell us your preferred route and we will confirm realistic options.
How do we arrange a COA and TDS before committing to an order?
A COA and TDS can be arranged on request; the testing scope is agreed per buyer specification. Share your marker targets, method preferences and any limits, and we will confirm with the producer which parameters can be documented before you place an order.
