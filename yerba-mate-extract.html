Yerba Mate Extract

Ilex paraguariensis — a naturally caffeinated leaf extract positioned around energy and focus formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Yerba mate extract is drawn from the leaf of Ilex paraguariensis and carries polyphenols, chlorogenic acids and naturally occurring caffeine. It has become a familiar name in natural-energy positioning, often paired with other botanical stimulants. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through vetted partner producers and confirm marker and caffeine targets honestly before quoting.

FAQ

Common questions.

What caffeine level can be specified in yerba mate extract, and is it naturally occurring?
Caffeine is naturally present in the leaf, so the extract carries it rather than having it added. Common requests range from 2% up to around 10%, expressed by HPLC. If your label declares caffeine, tell us the target number and we will confirm what our partner producers can match.
Is the extract standardized on polyphenols, chlorogenic acids, or both?
Polyphenols by UV is the most frequent single marker, with chlorogenic acids often added when the buyer wants a more specific phenolic anchor. Some buyers also request theobromine alongside caffeine. Share whichever combination your specification uses and we will check it against partner producer capability.
What documentation comes with a yerba mate extract order?
COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. Because caffeine content is regulated differently across markets, buyers often want that value clearly stated — let us know your required parameters and we will confirm what can be documented.
