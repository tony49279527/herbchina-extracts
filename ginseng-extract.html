Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Ginseng extract is a foundational adaptogen ingredient, sourced from Panax ginseng root and standardized to ginsenosides, the marker compound buyers reference in product claims and formulation. Demand spans capsules, shots, teas, and functional confectionery. As a trading company, we work with partner producers to match your target ginsenoside specification — we don't present stock specs or claim certifications we don't hold.

Specifications below reflect commonly requested options in the market — not a stock list. Share your target spec and we'll confirm exactly what we can supply, honestly.

FAQ

Common questions.

What ginsenoside spec options are commonly requested?
Market requests typically range from 10% to 80% ginsenosides, tested by HPLC or UV, with extract ratios also common for certain applications. Share your target and we'll confirm what can be supplied.
Can you match a custom ginsenoside specification?
Yes — send your target spec including marker content, test method, mesh size, and any documentation requirements. We'll confirm feasibility with our partner producers.
How is COA documentation handled?
COA and TDS can be arranged on request. The testing scope is agreed against your specification — we don't claim in-house lab testing. State your document requirements in the inquiry.
