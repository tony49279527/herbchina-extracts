Lychee Extract

Spray-dried fruit powder for beverage, confectionery and cosmetic formulations, polyphenol-positioned.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Lychee extract is a spray-dried fruit powder carrying the characteristic aroma and polyphenol profile of Litchi chinensis. It is positioned in beverages, confectionery and cosmetics where a fruit-forward identity is wanted. Buyers commonly specify polyphenol content or a carrier ratio such as 4:1 or 10:1. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through partner producers and confirm feasibility once your target spec is received.

FAQ

Common questions.

What plant part is used for lychee extract, and how is the powder produced?
The fruit, and in some cases fruit peel, is the typical raw material. Production generally involves aqueous extraction followed by spray drying onto a suitable carrier. Buyers specify the plant part and ratio grade, and we confirm these against your target specification.
What spec grades are commonly requested, and how are polyphenols measured?
Ratio grades such as 4:1 and 10:1 are common, along with polyphenol-positioned specifications. Polyphenols are typically measured by a colorimetric Folin-type method. Actual grades are confirmed on inquiry.
Do you supply COA and TDS, and how are custom flavour or colour targets handled?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. For custom specifications, we review your parameters with partner producers and confirm feasibility after receiving your detailed requirements.
