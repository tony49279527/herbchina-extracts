FAQ

Common questions.

Which grades of Huperzine A are commonly traded, and how is the marker tested?
The two grades most often requested are a 1% standardized extract and a 98%+ high-purity powder. Assay is typically confirmed by HPLC against a reference standard. The exact test method and acceptance criteria are agreed per buyer specification, and we confirm which grade the partner producer can offer for your target.
What plant part and source material is used for your Huperzine A?
The starting material is Huperzia serrata whole herb. Because Huperzine A content in raw herb varies, buyers often ask about the herb-to-extract ratio alongside the marker assay. Source material details and any ratio information are part of the documentation agreed per buyer specification.
What do you need from us to arrange COA/TDS and check supply feasibility?
Provide your target grade (1% or 98%+ or another level), assay method expectations, particle form and any purity limits. COA and TDS can be arranged on request; testing scope is agreed per buyer specification. We then confirm feasibility honestly with the vetted producer.
