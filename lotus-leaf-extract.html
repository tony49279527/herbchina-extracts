Lotus Leaf Extract

Lotus Leaf — a nuciferine-standardized leaf extract for weight-management and tea-blend formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Lotus leaf extract is produced from the leaf of Nelumbo nucifera and typically standardized to nuciferine, an alkaloid associated with the leaf's market positioning. Buyers commonly request grades from 2% to 10% for weight-management oriented supplements and tea blends. We operate as a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers and confirming each target nuciferine specification before quoting.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is nuciferine assayed by HPLC, and what range of grades is commonly traded?
Nuciferine is commonly measured by HPLC, with market grades typically falling between 2% and 10%. Because purity claims above this range are harder to substantiate, we ask buyers to state their target grade and method so we can confirm what vetted partner producers can realistically supply.
How do you check for adulteration, given nuciferine can be added as a pure compound?
Adulteration screening for lotus leaf extract usually involves checking whether nuciferine appears consistent with a genuine botanical matrix, often through additional chromatographic or fingerprint-style checks. Buyers should specify which checks they require, and we confirm the agreed testing scope with the producer.
How do we arrange documentation for a specific nuciferine grade?
Send your target nuciferine percentage and assay method, and we will confirm feasibility with partner producers. A COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with the testing scope agreed per buyer specification.
