FAQ

Common questions.

How is kojic acid assayed, and what related substances are typically monitored?
Assay is generally by HPLC against a reference standard, with related substances and residual solvents reviewed depending on the route of manufacture. Test scope is confirmed against your target specification; COA and TDS are available on request.
Why does color vary between kojic acid lots, and does it matter?
Kojic acid can darken on exposure to light, heat or trace metals, so color is often used as an informal stability indicator alongside assay. Buyers formulating light-colored products should specify color limits, and we will confirm what can be supported.
What formulation considerations apply when using kojic acid?
It is typically used at low percentages and can be sensitive to pH, heat and metal ions in the base, so compatibility with your system should be assessed. Share your intended use level and vehicle and we will confirm feasibility with partner producers.
