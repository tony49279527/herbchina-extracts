FAQ

Common questions.

How are beta-asarone-controlled grades defined and tested?
These grades specify either a maximum beta-asarone limit or a defined range agreed with the buyer, and GC or HPLC methods are commonly referenced in technical documentation. Because regulatory limits on beta-asarone differ by market, share your target limit and destination requirements for a feasibility review.
Which Acorus calamus material is used, and does variety matter?
The rhizome is the standard starting material for Vacha extract. Acorus calamus varieties and origins can differ in their natural beta-asarone profiles, which is why buyers typically state a limit; include your sourcing or variety preference in the inquiry if you have one.
Can you confirm compliance-related documentation for a target market?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. Tell us which parameters and limits your market requires and we will coordinate documentation review with our supply partners.
