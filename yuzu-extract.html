Yuzu Extract

Yuzu — citrus extract for beverage and immune-support formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Yuzu extract is a botanical powder derived from the fruit of Citrus junos, carrying the bright, aromatic profile favoured in citrus-flavour beverage concepts. Buyers commonly request grades standardised around hesperidin or vitamin C, depending on whether the target is flavour contribution or nutrient positioning. Demand continues to grow across Asian-inspired drinks, RTD teas and gummy formats. As a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers, we review each target specification against real production feasibility before quoting.

Specifications below reflect commonly requested options in the market — not a stock list. Share your target spec and we'll confirm exactly what we can supply, honestly.

FAQ

Common questions.

Which part of the yuzu is used and how is the extract standardised?
Material is sourced from Citrus junos fruit, with peel and juice fractions both traded depending on the flavour profile the buyer wants. Standardisation is usually built around hesperidin or vitamin C content, and the exact marker and analytical method can be aligned to your specification before we confirm feasibility with partner producers.
What hesperidin or vitamin C grades are commonly traded?
For yuzu, hesperidin grades in the 10-40% range are among the most frequently requested in the market, alongside vitamin C standardised options. These reflect common market practice rather than fixed inventory, and each order is matched to a producer able to meet your declared target spec.
How do we obtain a COA or TDS for yuzu extract?
A COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with the testing scope agreed per buyer specification. Because we trade rather than manufacture, documents are issued by the producing partner and reviewed against your requirements before release.
