What it is, and how we handle it.

FAQ

Common questions.

How is phaseolamin measured, and what grades are commonly requested?
Phaseolamin is typically reported as a percentage determined by an assay such as the DNS method or a comparable activity-based test, and 1-2% is the most commonly requested range. Because methods vary between producers, it is important to state the exact method you expect so results are comparable.
Can the extract be offered at higher phaseolamin levels?
Higher concentrations do appear in the market, but availability depends on the partner producer and current batch. Share the specific figure and method you need and we will confirm feasibility rather than quote an unavailable grade.
What can be provided for our specification review?
A COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. Let us know your target phaseolamin level, assay method, and any limits, and we will confirm what the producer can document.
