FAQ

Common questions.

How are apigenin and phthalides typically analysed in celery seed extract?
Apigenin is usually determined by HPLC, while phthalide content may be assessed by HPLC or GC depending on the specific compounds targeted. Buyers should specify which marker class they require, as it changes the analytical scope. These are commonly requested market approaches, with actual specs confirmed on inquiry.
Why is the seed used rather than other parts of Apium graveolens?
Celery seed is the conventional raw material for apigenin- and phthalide-standardised extracts, since the seed carries the characteristic volatile oil and flavonoid profile that buyers reference. Plant part should be declared in your specification, and we confirm it with partner producers during feasibility review.
What documentation is available with celery seed extract?
COA and TDS are available on request, and the testing scope is confirmed against your target specification. As a trading company we align documentation with the parameters you designate and verify feasibility with partner producers first.
