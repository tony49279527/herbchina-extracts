Peach Extract

Spray-dried fruit powder for beverage and flavour formulations with a peach identity.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Peach extract is a spray-dried fruit powder produced from Prunus persica, used mainly for its characteristic flavour and colour contribution in beverages and flavour systems. It is typically selected on ratio grade and carrier type, with water-soluble carriers common for beverage use. Demand follows growth in fruit-flavoured and better-for-you drinks. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through partner producers and confirm feasibility once your target spec is received.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is peach extract standardised to a marker compound, or mainly supplied on ratio?
Peach extract is most often supplied on a ratio basis rather than a single marker compound, since the primary value is flavour and colour. Where a marker specification is requested, it is agreed against your target spec and confirmed on inquiry.
What carrier is used in spray-dried peach extract, and how does it affect solubility?
Carriers such as maltodextrin or gum arabic are common for spray-dried fruit powders and influence solubility, colour and flow. Carrier choice is part of the specification, and we confirm the options against your formulation needs after receiving your brief.
Can you supply COA and TDS, and how are custom flavour grades handled?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. For custom specifications, we review your requirements with partner producers and confirm feasibility after receiving your detailed brief.
