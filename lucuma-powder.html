Lucuma Powder

Lucuma fruit powder — natural sweetness and fiber for food and beverage formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Lucuma Powder is milled from the dried fruit of Pouteria lucuma, a South American fruit prized for its mild caramel-like sweetness and dietary fiber content. Buyers evaluate it by fruit solids, sweetness profile, fiber and mesh size. Demand is rising in natural sweetener and functional food development. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through partner producers and confirm feasibility once your target spec is received.

FAQ

Common questions.

What part of the lucuma fruit is used for Lucuma Powder?
The powder is produced from the pulp of the ripe Pouteria lucuma fruit, which is dried and milled. Buyers sometimes ask about pulp-only vs whole-fruit material, and the source material is confirmed against your target spec before supply.
What mesh size and fiber content can be discussed for Lucuma Powder?
Commonly requested grades reference mesh size and dietary fiber level, both of which vary by application. Rather than quote a single stock figure, we map the requested mesh, fiber and sweetness profile against the partner producer's capability after receiving your target spec.
How is documentation handled for Lucuma Powder?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. Documentation is coordinated through our partner producers as a trading company.
