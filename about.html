Founded in 2019, HerbChina Extracts supplies botanical and mushroom extracts to overseas buyers who check what they buy.

Our story

The supplier we'd want to buy from.

Xi'an sits at the historic start of the Silk Road — a city where herbal trade has run for two thousand years. We founded HerbChina Extracts here in 2019 with a simple frustration: too many extract deals run on vague ratios and claims nobody can verify.

So we built the company we wanted to buy from. One where beta-glucan numbers are stated, not implied. Where fruiting body and mycelium aren't quietly blended. Where every batch comes with documentation your QA team can actually use.

Seven years in, that principle hasn't changed: if we can't verify it, we don't claim it.

What we believe

Three principles, no fine print.

🔍

Transparency first

Sources declared. Markers documented. If a buyer asks how we know, we show our work.

⚖️

Verifiable over claimable

We'd rather understate and prove it than overstate and hope nobody checks.

🤝

Responsive partnership

One contact point, straight answers, honest timelines — the way B2B should work.
