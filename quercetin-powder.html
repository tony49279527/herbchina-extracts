Quercetin Powder

Quercetin — an isolated flavonoid with strong immune and antioxidant positioning for capsule, powder and beverage formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Quercetin powder is an isolated flavonoid typically derived from Sophora japonica, standardised to 95–98% quercetin. Demand has grown strongly through 2024–2026, driven by immune and antioxidant-positioning supplements in capsule, powder and beverage formats. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through vetted partner producers and confirm each buyer's target specification honestly before quotation.

FAQ

Common questions.

What is the difference between quercetin 95% and 98%?
Both grades are widely traded, with 98% typically requested for premium or highly concentrated formulas and 95% for standard ranges. The right choice depends on your formulation and cost targets.
Is dihydrate or anhydrous quercetin available?
Both forms are traded in the market. The preferred form depends on your formulation and stability requirements; share your target and we will confirm what can be arranged.
How do you handle documentation?
A COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. We confirm what analytical data can be provided before order.
