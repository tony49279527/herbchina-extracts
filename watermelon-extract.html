Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Interest is growing among beverage brands building hydration-style and fruit-forward drink concepts where a soluble, shelf-stable powder is more practical than fresh juice. FAQ

Common questions.

Does the powder come from the flesh, rind or whole fruit?
Source material varies: flesh gives the higher L-citrulline association, while rind and whole-fruit streams can shift the lycopene and fiber profile. The material route is agreed against your target spec, since it materially changes the assay.
How are lycopene and L-citrulline tested?
Lycopene is typically measured by HPLC or spectrophotometric methods, while L-citrulline is generally quantified by HPLC or amino-acid analysis. Exact testing scope is confirmed against your target specification; COA and TDS are available on request.
What should we watch for when comparing watermelon powder offers?
Key checkpoints are whether the marker is naturally present or top-up fortified, carrier and spray-drying aids used, and consistency between lots. We disclose the declared route for any offer and confirm details once your target spec is received.
