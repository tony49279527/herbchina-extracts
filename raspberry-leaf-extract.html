Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Raspberry leaf extract is a straight botanical powder derived from Rubus idaeus leaf, standardized around its natural tannin and ellagitannin content. Demand has grown steadily alongside interest in traditional-women's-wellness and herbal tea blends. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source raspberry leaf extract through partner producers and confirm feasibility, grades and documentation only after reviewing your target spec.

FAQ

Common questions.

Which plant part does your raspberry leaf extract come from, and is it leaf only?
Our raspberry leaf extract is sourced from Rubus idaeus leaf material. Because raspberry leaf is often blended with cane or other leaf material in trade, we recommend specifying leaf-only sourcing and confirming the plant part on the COA against your target specification.
How is the tannin content typically tested for raspberry leaf extract?
Tannin grades are commonly measured by UV spectrophotometry, and some buyers request ellagitannin-related markers by HPLC. Because test method affects reported values, we confirm the agreed method against your target specification before quoting. COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec.
Can HerbChina supply raspberry leaf extract to a custom tannin specification?
Yes — share your target tannin content, test method and any medicinal-claim constraints, and we will confirm feasibility through partner producers and revert with a specification-matched quotation.
