FAQ

Common questions.

How is apigenin in parsley extract usually measured, and what should buyers specify?
Apigenin is typically quantified by HPLC and reported on a dried basis, sometimes alongside related flavonoids. Buyers should state whether they require apigenin specifically or total flavonoids, since the two lead to different analytical scopes. These are commonly requested market approaches and the actual spec is confirmed on inquiry.
Is chlorophyll-standardised parsley extract different from apigenin-standardised material?
Yes, they are distinct specification routes. Chlorophyll grades are usually assessed spectrophotometrically and are oriented toward colour and appearance, while apigenin grades are HPLC-based. The raw material and drying route can also differ. We confirm which marker route you need before sourcing.
Can COA and TDS be issued against our parsley extract target spec?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. As a trading company working with partner producers, we review your parameters first and confirm feasibility before documentation is provided.
