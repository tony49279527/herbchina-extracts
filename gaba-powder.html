Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

GABA (CAS 56-12-2) is a fermentation-derived amino acid commonly formulated into calm, relaxation and sleep-positioned supplements, often alongside L-theanine, magnesium or botanical actives. Buyers typically request 98%+ purity with a clean, low-colour powder profile for capsules, stick packs and gummies. Operating as a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers, we work from your target spec and confirm feasibility honestly before quotation.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is your GABA fermentation derived or chemically synthesised?
The material we source through partner producers is fermentation derived, which is the route most buyers request for label-friendly positioning. If your specification allows another production route, tell us and we will check feasibility accordingly.
Which assay methods are normally used for GABA, and what purity grades are common?
98%+ is the most commonly requested grade. HPLC and titration are both used for assay depending on buyer preference; state your preferred method and we confirm what partner producers can support.
How do I request a COA or TDS for GABA powder?
COA and TDS can be arranged on request; testing scope is agreed per buyer specification, so send us your required items and target spec first.
