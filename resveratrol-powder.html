Resveratrol Powder

Trans-resveratrol — a longevity and antioxidant-positioning stilbenoid for capsule, powder and cosmetic formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Resveratrol powder is a stilbenoid typically sourced from Polygonum cuspidatum root, standardised to trans-resveratrol. It is a key ingredient in longevity and antioxidant-positioned supplements and increasingly in skincare. 50% and 98–99% trans-resveratrol are the most commonly traded grades. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through vetted partner producers and confirm each buyer's target specification honestly.

FAQ

Common questions.

What is the difference between 50% and 98% trans-resveratrol?
50% trans-resveratrol is commonly used in standard antioxidant formulas, while 98–99% is requested for premium or highly concentrated products. The right grade depends on your formulation and labelling.
Why is trans-resveratrol specified rather than total resveratrol?
Trans-resveratrol is the more studied isomer and is the form most buyers specify for quality control. Assay is typically reported on a trans-resveratrol basis by HPLC.
How do you handle documentation?
A COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. We confirm the analytical package before quotation.
