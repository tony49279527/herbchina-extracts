Butterfly Pea Extract

Clitoria ternatea — an anthocyanin-rich flower extract for natural blue color and beverage formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Butterfly Pea Extract is produced from Clitoria ternatea flower and is valued for its anthocyanins and ternatins, which deliver a natural blue color. It has become a fast-growing ingredient in beverage, tea and color-focused concepts. HerbChina Extracts is a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers, matching color strength and marker content to your target specification.
