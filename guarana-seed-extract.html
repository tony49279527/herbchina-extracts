Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Guarana seed extract comes from the seeds of Paullinia cupana and is supplied as a tan to brownish powder, standardised on natural caffeine content or sold as a ratio extract. Beverage and sports-nutrition brands request it for energy drinks, shots and pre-workout blends where a naturally derived caffeine source suits the label. From Xi'an we handle guarana through vetted partner producers and confirm the caffeine grade against each buyer's target spec.

FAQ

Common questions.

What caffeine levels are typical, and how is the caffeine confirmed?
Standardised grades commonly requested run from about 10% to 22% natural caffeine, with 22% being a frequent target for beverage applications, while 4:1 and 10:1 ratio extracts are traded for label-driven formats. Caffeine is typically confirmed by HPLC. Because the ratio grades carry lower and less predictable caffeine, buyers should state which route they need; the test panel is agreed per buyer specification.
How do buyers distinguish guarana from added synthetic caffeine?
This is a key adulteration checkpoint. Buyers commonly request a caffeine assay alongside supporting markers such as theobromine and theophylline, plus tannin or other guarana-derived values, since a genuine seed extract carries a broader alkaloid profile than straight caffeine. Documentation and method details are agreed case by case; COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification.
Which part of the plant is used, and does the extract contain added carriers?
The extract is taken from the seed. Standardised powders are sometimes offered with a carrier such as maltodextrin to reach the stated caffeine figure, which matters for buyers counting total caffeine per serving or managing allergen and labelling declarations. Tell us whether carrier-free is required, along with your caffeine target, and we will confirm what the partner producer can supply.
