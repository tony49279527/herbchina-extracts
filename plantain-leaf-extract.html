What it is, and how we handle it.

FAQ

Common questions.

How is aucubin in plantain leaf extract typically determined?
Aucubin is commonly quantified by HPLC using an appropriate reference standard, and results are typically expressed on a dried basis. Total-flavonoid grades are usually measured by colorimetric methods. Because producer methods vary, we confirm the analytical approach against your target specification before sourcing.
Which part of Plantago major is used, and can you distinguish it from other Plantago species?
The leaf is the standard raw material for Plantago major extracts. Because other Plantago species are also traded, buyers often include species verification and, where relevant, marker profiles in their specification. We confirm the declared species and plant part with partner producers during feasibility review.
What documentation can you provide with plantain leaf extract?
COA and TDS are available on request, and the testing scope is confirmed against your target spec. As a trading company we align documentation with the parameters you designate and verify feasibility with partner producers first.
