Magnesium Glycinate Powder

Magnesium bisglycinate — chelated mineral powder positioned for sleep, calm and daily mineral formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Magnesium glycinate is the chelate of magnesium with glycine, supplied as a white, free-flowing powder and specified by elemental magnesium content. Supplement brands request it for sleep- and calm-positioned capsules, stick packs and drink mixes because the chelated form is well tolerated and mixes cleanly. From Xi'an we trade this material through vetted partner producers, matching each buyer's required elemental magnesium range and presentation.

FAQ

Common questions.

What elemental magnesium content should we expect, and how is it tested?
Grades commonly requested in the market run from about 10% to 18% elemental magnesium, with 14% and higher typically associated with fully chelated material. Elemental magnesium is normally determined by titration or ICP, and buyers often ask for supporting evidence that the magnesium is genuinely chelated rather than a blend of magnesium oxide or sulfate with free glycine. The test panel and acceptance range are agreed per buyer specification.
How can we tell a true chelate from a physical blend with glycine?
This is one of the main adulteration checkpoints for this material. Buyers commonly review the ratio of magnesium to glycine against theoretical chelate values, check nitrogen content, look at solubility and particle behaviour, and where available request chelation-specific testing. We raise these questions with our partner producers and share what documentation is realistically available; the verification approach is settled per buyer specification.
Is a low-dust or granulated grade available, and what about COA and TDS?
Powder grades vary in bulk density and dustiness, and some buyers specify a granulated or agglomerated form for easier handling in stick packs. Tell us your preferred presentation, elemental magnesium target and packaging, and we will confirm feasibility with the partner producer. COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification.
