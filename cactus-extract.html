Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Cactus extract is derived from the cladodes and fruit of Opuntia ficus-indica and is used for its natural polysaccharide and polyphenol profile. Buyers commonly request 10:1 and 20:1 powdered grades for capsules, powders and functional beverages. Incoming enquiry clusters around the hydration and weight-management categories. FAQ

Common questions.

Does cactus extract use cladode, fruit, or both — and how does that affect the spec?
Both cladode and fruit feedstocks are traded, and the ratio grades can differ by starting material. Cladode material is often linked to polysaccharide and fiber content, while fruit carries more color and polyphenols, so the plant part should be named in your specification.
How do buyers verify the ratio and antioxidant profile of cactus extract?
Ratio is typically stated as w/w plant material to extract, while polyphenol or antioxidant values may be tested by colorimetric assays against a reference standard. Because methods vary, agreeing the analytical approach early keeps COA figures comparable across lots.
Can you match a custom cactus extract spec and supply documents?
Yes, share your target ratio, plant part, particle size and packing, and we will confirm feasibility with our vetted partner producers. COA and TDS can be arranged on request; testing scope is agreed per buyer specification.
