What it is, and how we handle it.

Buyers commonly request 4:1, 10:1 and 20:1 ratio grades for juice-powder concepts, drink mixes and wellness blends. FAQ

Common questions.

What fruit material is used to make Noni Extract?
The fruit of Morinda citrifolia is the starting material, typically harvested at maturity. Extraction is generally carried out with water or aqueous alcohol followed by concentration and spray drying. If you need a specific fruit maturity or juice-based input, share it and we will confirm feasibility with the partner producer.
Are 4:1, 10:1 and 20:1 Noni grades interchangeable?
No. Higher ratios represent more concentrated powder on an input-to-output basis and suit smaller-dose formats, while lower ratios are often chosen for drink mixes where a higher inclusion level is acceptable. Buyers should specify the ratio and any carrier, and a COA can be arranged on request with testing scope agreed per buyer specification.
Does the extract retain the characteristic noni aroma and flavour?
Sensory profile varies with extraction method, concentration, and carrier. Some buyers want a more neutral powder for flavoured blends; others prefer a closer-to-fruit profile. Let us know your target and we will confirm what the partner producer can realistically deliver.
