Neem Extract

Neem — a classic Ayurvedic leaf botanical for skin-care and detox-positioned formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Neem extract is a botanical powder derived from the leaves of Azadirachta indica, valued in supplement and cosmetic development for its bitter principles and azadirachtin-type limonoids. Overseas buyers typically request either standardized azadirachtin grades or total neem-bitters ratios depending on the intended formulation. As a trading company based in Xi'an, we work through partner producers and confirm sourcing feasibility once a buyer's target specification is received.

Specifications below reflect commonly requested options in the market — not a stock list. Share your target spec and we'll confirm exactly what we can supply, honestly.

FAQ

Common questions.

What azadirachtin content can be supplied, and how is it tested?
Azadirachtin grades are among the most commonly requested for neem leaf extract, typically expressed as a percentage and analyzed by HPLC against a reference standard. Because achievable content depends on leaf quality, harvest timing and the concentration process, we confirm the feasible range only after receiving your target spec. Test method and acceptance criteria are agreed before quotation.
Which plant part is used, and can you supply leaf-only material?
Neem extract for supplement and cosmetic use is normally produced from the leaf, though some supply chains blend leaf with other plant parts. If your specification requires leaf-only sourcing, state this clearly, since it affects both marker profile and documentation. We relay the requirement to partner producers and confirm what the current supply can realistically meet.
How do I get a COA or TDS for a specific neem grade?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. Share your required marker, method and any limits, and we will match them to what partner producers can document.
