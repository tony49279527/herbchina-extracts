Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Bitter orange extract is produced from the fruit of Citrus aurantium and standardized on synephrine, commonly at 6-10%. It appears in energy and weight-management supplement concepts, sometimes in combination with other botanicals. FAQ

Common questions.

What synephrine levels are commonly requested, and how is the marker tested?
6-10% is the range most buyers reference, with 10% often requested for concentrated capsule lines. Synephrine is usually determined by HPLC, and some buyers also ask about related alkaloids such as octopamine. State your target and method, and we will confirm what the partner producer can meet.
Is the extract standardized from the whole fruit or specific plant parts?
The material is derived from the fruit of Citrus aurantium, and the synephrine content in the raw fruit varies with source and maturity. Because of that natural variation, buyers specify a tested marker percentage rather than relying on the plant part alone. We can confirm how the partner producer standardizes to your target.
What quality documents can be arranged for this product?
A COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. Provide your target synephrine level, method, and any additional limits, and we will confirm what can be documented.
