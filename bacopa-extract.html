Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Bacopa Extract is an aerial-part botanical standardized to bacosides, a key marker for nootropic and cognitive wellness positioning. It is widely used in capsule, powder, and functional beverage concepts. As a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers, HerbChina Extracts helps overseas supplement, food, beverage, and cosmetics brands compare commonly requested market specifications. Buyers share their target spec and we confirm feasibility honestly before quotation.
