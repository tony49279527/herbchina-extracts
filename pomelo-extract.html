Pomelo Extract

Spray-dried pomelo fruit powder positioned around naringin for beverage and functional-food development.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Pomelo extract is a spray-dried citrus fruit powder derived from Citrus maxima, processed to retain the fruit's native flavonoid profile and positioned around naringin as the characteristic marker. Formulators working on citrus-forward beverages, powdered drink mixes and functional foods increasingly ask for a pumpable, free-flowing powder rather than fresh fruit solids. As a trading company in Xi'an we source through vetted partner producers; feasibility is confirmed once we receive your target specification.

FAQ

Common questions.

Which part of the pomelo fruit is used, and how is the powder produced?
Material is typically the whole fruit or peel/juice solids depending on the target marker profile, and powder is produced by spray-drying a strained fruit preparation. Because naringin sits mostly in the peel and membrane, the plant part mix directly affects assay — tell us your target spec and we will confirm which material route fits.
How is naringin content analyzed?
Naringin is normally quantified by HPLC against a reference standard, often alongside total flavonoid or UV-based methods. Testing scope is confirmed against your target spec rather than assumed, and COA/TDS are available on request.
Can you support a custom carrier or dilution level?
Carrier systems and dilution levels are part of the specification discussion. Share your target naringin range, preferred carrier and end application, and we will confirm feasibility through our partner producers.
