Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Serrapeptase is a proteolytic enzyme produced by fermentation, supplied to supplement manufacturers in activity-graded powders measured in SPU. Formulators select activity grades based on dosage format, with enteric and non-enteric options commonly discussed. Demand tracks growth in enzyme-based supplement categories. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through partner producers and confirm feasibility once your target spec is received.

FAQ

Common questions.

What SPU activity grades of serrapeptase are commonly requested, and how is activity measured?
Buyers most often specify activity in SPU, with grades selected according to dose format and target label claims. Activity is typically determined by a protease assay against a defined substrate, with the method agreed to your specification. Actual grades are confirmed on inquiry.
How is serrapeptase produced, and what should buyers check on the COA?
It is produced by fermentation of Serratia species followed by recovery and standardisation to activity. Buyers commonly check activity value, carrier or diluent, moisture, and microbial limits. Testing scope is confirmed against your target spec.
Can you provide COA and TDS, and how are custom activity specs handled?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. For custom activity grades, we review your parameters with partner producers and confirm feasibility after receiving your brief.
