FAQ

Common questions.

Is pterostilbene supplied as a natural extract or a synthetic stilbenoid, and how is that reflected in the spec?
Both natural-isolation and synthetic routes appear in the market. Buyers often specify which route they require, since it can influence the impurity profile, residual solvent picture and documentation. We confirm what the partner producer actually supplies before quoting so the spec is realistic.
How is 98–99% pterostilbene verified, and what adulteration checks apply?
Purity is commonly determined by HPLC, often with a related-substance panel and residual solvent testing. Adulteration checkpoints buyers raise include substitution or blending with resveratrol, undeclared diluents and assay-versus-purity inconsistencies. The exact panel is agreed per buyer specification.
Can a COA/TDS be provided against my target purity?
Yes — a COA/TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. Share your target pterostilbene purity, source route preference and required test panel, and we will confirm feasibility honestly through our vetted partner producers.
