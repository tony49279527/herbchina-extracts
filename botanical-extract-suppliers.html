Buyer guide

How to evaluate a botanical extract supplier.

Price matters, but the expensive mistakes in extract sourcing are documentation and consistency problems. This is the checklist we recommend running against any supplier — including us.

Documentation and COA. Ask for a certificate of analysis for a real batch, not a generic spec sheet. A useful COA states the specification, the test methods, and the result for that batch. Ask whether a TDS and MSDS are available.
Batch consistency. Ask how the supplier controls variability between lots: incoming raw material inspection, in-process controls, and finished-goods testing against a fixed specification range.
Traceability. Can the supplier name the plant species, plant part used, and origin of the raw material? Ask whether raw material lots are recorded against finished batch numbers.
Testing scope. Ask what is tested and against which specification — and get the scope agreed in writing before supply, not after.

FAQ

Common questions.

How do I verify a botanical extract supplier?
Start with documentation for a real batch: COA, specification sheet, and MSDS. Ask about raw material traceability and batch consistency controls. If possible, order a sample and evaluate it against your own specification before committing to volume.
What are your minimum order quantities?
MOQs are flexible and depend on the extract and specification. Tell us the material and the volume you are planning and we will confirm what is feasible.
Can I get samples before ordering?
Yes. Samples can be arranged on request, subject to the material and the quantity needed for your evaluation.
Do you ship worldwide?
Yes. We export from China to customers internationally. Shipping method and documentation are agreed per order.
Are certificates of analysis provided?
COA/TDS documents can be arranged on request. The testing scope is agreed against your specification so the documents match what you need.
Can you produce to a custom specification?
In many cases, yes. Share the specification and target application and we will confirm feasibility with our partner producers.
