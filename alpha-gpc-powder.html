Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Alpha-GPC (L-alpha-glycerylphosphorylcholine) is a choline-containing compound widely used across cognitive-support and sports-nutrition categories, where it frequently appears in pre-workout and nootropic blends. It is commonly offered in 50% and 99% grades, with the higher grade favored for compact-dose formats. FAQ

Common questions.

What is the difference between 50% and 99% Alpha-GPC grades?
The 50% grade is typically a diluted or carrier-based form, while the 99% grade is a higher-purity material. Buyers choose between them based on dose size, capsule fill constraints and label positioning. State your target grade and we will confirm what a partner producer can offer for that specification.
Is Alpha-GPC offered as the L-alpha isomer, and how is identification confirmed?
The ingredient is specified as L-alpha-glycerylphosphorylcholine, the isomer most buyers require. Identity and assay are typically confirmed by HPLC or a comparable chromatographic method. Buyers should confirm the assay method and any isomer-specific requirements before agreeing a specification.
What documentation is available for Alpha-GPC?
A COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. Send your target grade, assay method and any restricted-substance limits, and we will confirm feasibility before quoting.
