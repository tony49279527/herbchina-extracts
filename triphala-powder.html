Triphala — classical three-fruit Ayurvedic blend powder for digestive-wellness and herbal capsule formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Triphala is a traditional three-fruit blend of Emblica officinalis, Terminalia bellirica and Terminalia chebula, traded as a fine powder or as extract grades for herbal and digestive-wellness formats. Overseas supplement brands continue to request it for Ayurvedic-style blends and multi-herb capsules. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through vetted partner producers and match each buyer's target mesh, ratio or extract spec before quoting.

FAQ

Common questions.

Which plant parts are used and in what ratio?
The classical blend uses the dried fruit of all three species. Buyers specify the fruit ratio (commonly equal parts) and whether they want straight ground fruit powder or a concentrated extract grade; we confirm feasibility against your target spec before quoting.
What spec options are commonly requested for Triphala?
Most inquiries are for straight fruit powder at a defined mesh size, or for extract grades described by ratio (e.g. 4:1 to 10:1) or tannin content. COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification.
How is the blend verified for identity?
Buyers typically ask for identity confirmation of each of the three fruit components plus a marker or ratio check. The specific test panel is agreed per buyer specification, and supporting documentation can be arranged on request through our partner producers.
