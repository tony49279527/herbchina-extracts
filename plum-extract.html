Plum Extract

Spray-dried fruit powder for beverage and functional-food formulations, phenolic-positioned.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Plum extract is a spray-dried fruit powder derived from Prunus salicina, positioned around its phenolic content and deep fruit colour. It suits beverage and functional-food applications where a plum identity and anthocyanin-related colour are useful. Buyers commonly request ratio grades or phenolic-positioned specifications. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through partner producers and confirm feasibility once your target spec is received.

FAQ

Common questions.

Which Prunus salicina material is used, and does the extract carry natural colour?
The fruit is the typical raw material, and plum extracts can contribute natural colour related to their anthocyanin content. Colour intensity varies with ratio grade and carrier. Specifics are confirmed against your target specification.
What ratio or phenolic grades are commonly requested, and how is the powder standardised?
Ratio specifications such as 4:1 and 10:1 are common, as are phenolic-positioned grades standardised by colorimetric assay. Standardisation method and value are confirmed on inquiry.
What documents accompany the material, and how do custom spec requests proceed?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. For custom requirements, we review your parameters with partner producers and confirm feasibility after receiving your brief.
