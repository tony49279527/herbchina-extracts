Acacia Fiber Powder

Acacia Fiber — a gentle soluble-fiber gum positioned for prebiotic and digestive-comfort formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Acacia fiber is the dried gum exudate of Acacia senegal, valued in supplement and food design for its high soluble-fiber content and low viscosity in use. Buyers commonly request 90%+ soluble fiber grades. Interest has tracked the broader prebiotic and low-FODMAP-friendly formulation trend. As a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers, we handle grades as a powder matched to your target specification.

FAQ

Common questions.

How is soluble fiber content in acacia gum typically verified by buyers?
Most buyers request soluble fiber by AOAC-type enzymatic-gravimetric methods or a total dietary fiber assay, with the agreed method named on the COA. Because gum grades can vary in fiber versus residual moisture and insoluble matter, we recommend confirming test method early so the spec sheet and certificate line up.
What is the source material and how is the gum collected?
The material is the natural gum exudate tapped from Acacia senegal trees, then cleaned, milled and sieved. Buyers sometimes ask about whether the gum is spray-dried or simply milled, since particle size and flow behavior differ between the two for capsule versus beverage use.
Can you supply acacia fiber against a custom spec, and what documentation is available?
Yes — share your target soluble fiber percentage, particle size and any preferred test method, and we will confirm feasibility honestly with our vetted partner producers. COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification.
