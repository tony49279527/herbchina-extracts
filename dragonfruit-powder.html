Dragonfruit Powder

Dragonfruit powder — natural color and fiber for food and beverage formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Dragonfruit Powder is produced from the fruit of Hylocereus undatus, commonly used for its natural color contribution and dietary fiber. Buyers evaluate color intensity, betacyanin presence, fiber and mesh size. Demand has grown in clean-label color and functional beverage development. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through partner producers and confirm feasibility after receiving your target spec.

FAQ

Common questions.

Does Dragonfruit Powder use white-flesh or red-flesh fruit?
Both white-flesh and red-flesh Hylocereus undatus material are traded, and the choice affects color intensity and betacyanin profile. Source material variety and flesh type are confirmed against your target spec before supply.
How is color evaluated for Dragonfruit Powder?
Buyers often reference color value, betacyanin content and visual hue, sometimes supported by spectrophotometric measurement. Since these parameters vary by fruit and drying method, requested color grades are mapped to partner producer capability after receiving your target spec.
What documentation is available for Dragonfruit Powder?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. Documentation is arranged through our partner producers.
