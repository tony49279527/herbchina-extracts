FAQ

Common questions.

Should I specify an extract ratio or an anthocyanin level, and how is each verified?
Both approaches are traded. Extract-ratio grades state the botanical-to-extract relationship, while anthocyanin-standardized grades state a measured content, commonly by UV or HPLC. Because ratio grades do not by themselves define active content, buyers often pair a ratio with an anthocyanin figure; the reporting basis is agreed per buyer specification.
What carrier systems are common, and how do buyers check for dilution?
Spray-dried acai grades often use a carrier such as maltodextrin or gum arabic, and buyers frequently ask for the carrier type and percentage to be declared. Adulteration checkpoints include undeclared carriers, addition of other anthocyanin-rich fruit sources and mismatched color versus measured anthocyanins; the checks performed are agreed with your specification.
What do you need to quote against my target grade?
Send your target ratio or anthocyanin level, preferred carrier system and required test panel. We will confirm feasibility honestly through our vetted partner producers, and a COA/TDS can be arranged on request with testing scope agreed per buyer specification.
