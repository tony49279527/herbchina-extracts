Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Caffeine powder is a widely used active in energy, pre-workout and sports nutrition formulations, typically supplied at high purity grades. It can be derived from tea leaf or coffee bean, and buyers often specify the botanical source. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source caffeine through partner producers and confirm source, grade and documentation feasibility once your target specification is received.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is your caffeine sourced from tea or coffee bean, and can the botanical origin be specified?
Both tea (Camellia sinensis) and coffee bean (Coffea arabica) are common source materials. Buyers can specify their preferred source in the target spec, and we confirm availability with partner producers on inquiry.
What purity grades of caffeine are commonly requested for supplement applications?
The 99% caffeine content grade is the most commonly requested for supplement and beverage applications, though lower grades are also traded. Exact grade is confirmed against your target specification.
Can you supply COA and TDS for caffeine powder, and what does the testing scope cover?
COA and TDS are available on request, and the testing scope is confirmed against your target spec. We do not fix a testing panel until we understand your required parameters.
