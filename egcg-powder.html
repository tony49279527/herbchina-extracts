FAQ

Common questions.

What assay grades and test methods are commonly requested for EGCG powder?
Most buyers ask for 90%, 95% or 98% EGCG by HPLC, with total catechins sometimes reported alongside. Some buyers also request a specified caffeine ceiling. Because assay methods and reference standards vary between labs, we agree the test method with the buyer before confirming a spec.
What starting material and part is used, and does the source affect the spec?
EGCG is extracted from Camellia sinensis leaf. Green tea, white tea and other leaf grades can be used depending on the producer, and the leaf profile can influence residual caffeine, catechin distribution and color. Buyers with tight caffeine or ratio requirements should share those limits up front.
Can you supply EGCG powder against my custom specification and provide documentation?
Yes. Share your target assay, test method, caffeine limit, particle size and any carrier requirements, and we will confirm feasibility with vetted partner producers honestly. A COA can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification.
