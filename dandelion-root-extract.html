Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Dandelion root is a widely traded botanical in liver-support, detox and digestive wellness categories. Extracts are commonly supplied as ratio forms such as 4:1 or 10:1, with inulin and flavonoid standardization also requested. We source through vetted partner producers and confirm each buyer's ratio, marker and carrier before quotation.
