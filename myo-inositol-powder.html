Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Myo-inositol is a high-volume active positioned in metabolic and women's-health supplement lines, typically supplied as a white crystalline powder with good water solubility and a mildly sweet taste. Buyers commonly request 99%+ purity, and many require a free-flowing, low-dust grade for stick packs and drink mixes. As a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers, we work from your target spec and confirm feasibility honestly.

FAQ

Common questions.

What is the difference between myo-inositol and D-chiro-inositol?
They are distinct isomers, and buyers frequently combine them at specific ratios in women's-health formulations. We source myo-inositol; if your formulation calls for a blend or a specific ratio, share the details and we will confirm partner-producer feasibility.
Which purity and test method are commonly requested for myo-inositol?
99%+ by HPLC is the most commonly requested grade. If your specification calls for a different method or limit, state it and we confirm what can realistically be supported.
Can you arrange a COA or TDS for myo-inositol powder?
COA and TDS can be arranged on request; testing scope is agreed per buyer specification, so send your required items together with your target spec.
