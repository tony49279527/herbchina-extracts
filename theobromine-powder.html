Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Theobromine is a naturally occurring xanthine alkaloid found in cacao (Theobroma cacao), used by formulators building energy, focus and pre-workout concepts as a milder alternative to caffeine-led profiles. It appears in capsules, RTD beverages and powdered drink mixes. FAQ

Common questions.

What is the difference between theobromine and caffeine in a formulation context?
Both are xanthine alkaloids, but theobromine is structurally distinct and is generally positioned in formulas wanting a gentler stimulant profile than caffeine. Buyers often ask us to confirm residual caffeine levels where the label needs to state theobromine specifically, so the assay scope should reflect that.
How is theobromine content verified and what purity checks are standard?
Content is typically determined by HPLC, with 98% being the most commonly requested grade in trade. Buyers frequently request related xanthine profiling to confirm the material is theobromine-dominant rather than a mixed alkaloid fraction. Testing scope is confirmed against your target spec on inquiry.
What source material is your theobromine produced from?
Theobromine is obtained from cacao (Theobroma cacao) sources, most often as a co-product of cocoa processing, with the extract then purified to the required alkaloid content. Source specifics and documentation are confirmed once we have your target specification.
