FAQ

Common questions.

How is IgG content measured in Bovine Colostrum Powder?
IgG content is typically measured by immunoassay methods such as ELISA or radial immunodiffusion, depending on the supplier and the agreed testing scope. Common market grades fall in the 20-40% range. Buyers should specify the test method they expect, and a COA can be arranged on request with testing scope agreed per buyer specification.
What is the source material and how is the powder processed?
The starting material is bovine first milk collected in the first milkings after calving. Processing generally involves separation, low-temperature pasteurisation, and drying to preserve the protein fraction. If you require a specific processing temperature or protein content, share it and we will confirm feasibility with the partner producer.
Can I request a specification with a specific IgG level and protein content?
Yes. Share your target IgG percentage, protein content, moisture limit and any microbiological requirements. We pass the specification to vetted partner producers and confirm feasibility honestly, with documentation arranged on request.
