Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Cassia Seed Extract is derived from the seeds of Cassia obtusifolia, a plant used in traditional Chinese herbal practice. The extract is commonly traded standardized to anthraquinone content and appears in herbal-tea and digestive-wellness concepts. Demand is steady in Asian-inspired functional beverage and tea categories. HerbChina Extracts is a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers; buyers share a target spec and we confirm feasibility honestly before any commitment.

FAQ

Common questions.

How is Cassia Seed Extract typically standardized?
Market grades are commonly standardized to anthraquinone content, with ratio extracts such as 10:1 also traded. The exact marker and test method should be agreed per buyer specification; COA/TDS can be arranged on request and testing scope is agreed per buyer specification.
Which plant part is your cassia material sourced from?
The material is sourced from the seed of Cassia obtusifolia, which is the part most commonly traded for tea and herbal applications. Buyers should specify seed-derived material, as leaf and other plant parts are sometimes seen in the market.
What is the process for confirming a custom cassia seed specification?
Share your target marker, assay range, test method and intended application. As a trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers, we check feasibility against your spec and confirm honestly what can be matched.
