Monk Fruit Extract

Siraitia grosvenorii — a mogroside V-standardized fruit extract for natural sweetener formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Monk Fruit Extract is derived from Siraitia grosvenorii fruit, native to China, and is standardized to mogroside V. It is a leading natural high-intensity sweetener used in beverages, foods and supplements. As a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers, we align mogroside V content, purity and format with your target specification.

Specifications below reflect commonly requested options in the market — not a stock list. Share your target spec and we'll confirm exactly what we can supply, honestly.
