FAQ

Common questions.

What does alkaloid-controlled mean for Bala extract specifications?
It generally refers to grades where total alkaloid content is standardized or limited to a defined range agreed with the buyer. Analytical approaches vary, so share your target alkaloid range and preferred test method so the specification can be checked with our supply partners.
Which plant part is extracted, and how does that affect the grade?
Sida cordifolia root is the conventional starting material for Bala extract. Root-sourced material is typically standardized to the marker or alkaloid range in the buyer's specification; if you require root-only sourcing, state that in your inquiry.
What documentation is provided, and when?
COA and TDS are available on request, and testing scope is confirmed against your target spec. Provide the parameters your quality team needs and we will coordinate the documentation review process.
