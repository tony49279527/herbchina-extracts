Tamarind Seed Extract

Tamarind Seed — seed-derived botanical for food, beverage and cosmetic formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Tamarind seed extract is derived from Tamarindus indica seed and is typically standardized around xyloglucan, the polysaccharide marker most buyers reference. It draws demand across food, beverage and cosmetic formulations for its functional and texturizing role. As a Xi'an trading company, we source through partner producers and confirm xyloglucan grades and documentation once the buyer's target spec is received.

FAQ

Common questions.

How is xyloglucan content assessed in tamarind seed extract, and what grades are commonly requested?
Xyloglucan is typically assessed by methods such as HPLC or gravimetric analysis, and buyers commonly request standardized xyloglucan grades. Exact percentages and test methods are confirmed on inquiry against your target spec, verified through partner producers.
Is the extract made from the seed kernel, and how does processing affect functionality?
Offers are based on Tamarindus indica seed, typically the kernel, with processing affecting viscosity and functional behavior. Production details vary by partner producer, so they are confirmed after we receive your target specification.
What documentation is available for a tamarind seed extract order?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. As a trading company sourcing through partner producers, we confirm the applicable documentation once your requirements are clear.
