Oat Straw Extract

Oat straw — aerial-part extract for calming and skin-care formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Oat straw extract is a powder produced from the aerial parts of Avena sativa, traded for its avenacoside and flavonoid content. Formulators position it in calming-oriented supplement concepts and in topical skin-care ranges where oat actives are well recognised by consumers. Specification is usually built around an extract ratio or a declared avenacoside level. As a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers, we take your target marker and application format and confirm what is realistically achievable.

FAQ

Common questions.

Which plant part is used, and does it affect avenacoside content?
The reference material is oat straw, meaning the aerial parts of Avena sativa rather than the grain. Avenacoside levels differ between straw and other fractions, so we verify plant part with the partner producer as part of the specification review before confirming feasibility.
What extract ratios and flavonoid grades are commonly traded?
Extract ratios from roughly 10:1 to 20:1 are among the most frequently requested options, alongside grades standardised to avenacosides or total flavonoids. These represent common market practice rather than guaranteed availability, and each enquiry is matched to a producer able to meet your declared target.
How are COA and TDS handled for oat straw extract?
A COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with the testing scope agreed per buyer specification. Let us know the marker, method and limits you require, and we will confirm what the producing partner can provide.
