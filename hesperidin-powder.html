Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Hesperidin is a flavone glycoside occurring in citrus peel and related tissues, widely traded as an off-white to pale tan powder. Buyers specify it for citrus-flavonoid blends, capsules and functional beverage premixes. As a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers, we take your target assay, test method and particle requirements, confirm feasibility honestly, and coordinate supply and documentation accordingly.

FAQ

Common questions.

Which test method is typically used for hesperidin assay, and does it match what our QC runs?
HPLC is the most commonly requested assay method for hesperidin, and many buyers also ask for a UV-based check as a secondary value. Because results can differ between methods, we ask you to state your preferred method up front; the testing scope is then agreed per buyer specification and can be arranged on request.
What plant part does the hesperidin in your supply chain typically come from, and does that matter for spec?
Hesperidin is most often obtained from citrus peel, particularly immature or unripe peel fractions, though citrus species and harvest stage vary by producer. This can influence residual sugars, color and the ratio of related flavonoids such as hesperetin. If your formulation is sensitive to these, share the parameters you care about and we will confirm what the partner producer can realistically hold.
Can you supply a custom assay or a blend rather than a straight single-marker grade?
Yes, in principle. For non-standard assays, carrier-diluted grades, or premixes combining hesperidin with other citrus flavonoids, send your target specification and intended use. We confirm feasibility with the partner producer honestly rather than assuming, and COA/TDS documentation can be arranged on request once the spec is settled.
