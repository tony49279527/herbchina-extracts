FAQ

Common questions.

What grades of ascorbic acid are most commonly traded, and how is content verified?
The 99% grade is the most commonly requested in bulk trade, and content is usually verified by titration or HPLC against an L-ascorbic acid standard. Buyers often also ask for related degradation markers such as dehydroascorbic acid to be checked. Testing scope is confirmed against your target spec on inquiry.
What is the difference between ascorbic acid and other vitamin C forms?
Ascorbic acid is the free acid form of vitamin C, whereas mineral salts such as sodium ascorbate or calcium ascorbate are buffered and less acidic. Which form fits depends on your finished format and label position. We can confirm what is feasible from our partner producers once we have your target spec.
What documentation can you supply for ascorbic acid orders?
COA and TDS are available on request, with the testing scope confirmed against your target spec. If you require specific assay, heavy metal or stability parameters, include them with your inquiry so we can confirm alignment before quoting.
