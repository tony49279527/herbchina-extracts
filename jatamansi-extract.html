FAQ

Common questions.

How is valeranone content verified in jatamansi extract?
Valeranone is a sesquiterpene ketone typically measured by gas chromatography or HPLC, depending on the analytical setup. Specifications usually state a standardized percentage, and some buyers also reference total volatile or sesquiterpene content. We confirm the achievable range only after reviewing your target specification.
Why does jatamansi root sourcing matter for quality?
Nardostachys jatamansi is a slow-growing Himalayan species, and root quality, age and harvest practices influence both the aromatic profile and valeranone content. Because the species is subject to conservation attention in some regions, buyers should confirm origin and chain-of-custody expectations in their specification. We relay these requirements to partner producers for feasibility review.
Can a custom jatamansi specification be matched?
Custom specifications can be discussed, and feasibility is confirmed after you share your target spec. COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your requirements.
