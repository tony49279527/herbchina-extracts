What it is, and how we handle it.

FAQ

Common questions.

How are sennosides measured in senna leaf extract, and which grades do buyers commonly request?
Sennosides A and B are typically determined by HPLC or UV methods, and buyers commonly request standardized sennoside percentages. Exact figures and test method are confirmed on inquiry against your target spec, with feasibility checked through partner producers.

Offers are based on Senna alexandrina leaf. Adulteration checkpoints usually include marker identity testing and botanical authentication, with the actual testing scope confirmed against your target spec because sourcing runs through partner producers.
