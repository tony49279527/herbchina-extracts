FAQ

Common questions.

What is the difference between niacinamide and nicotinic acid for formulation purposes?
Both are vitamin B3 forms, but niacinamide is the amide and is the form normally specified in cosmetic and supplement briefs. Buyers should confirm the declared form, since identity testing distinguishes the two and affects the finished label.
How is purity verified, and what impurities are usually checked?
Assay is typically by HPLC or titration, with related substances such as nicotinic acid monitored as an impurity marker. Exact test scope is confirmed against your target specification; COA and TDS are available on request.
Are there moisture or storage considerations for high-purity niacinamide?
Niacinamide is hygroscopic, so moisture content and packaging protection are relevant to long-term stability and caking behavior. Share your storage and handling assumptions and we will confirm what the partner production can meet.
