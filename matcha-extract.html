Matcha extract — green tea leaf extract for beverage and food formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Matcha extract is produced from shade-grown Camellia sinensis leaves and valued for its vivid green color together with EGCG and caffeine content. Beverage and food brands use it as a concentrated green-tea flavor and color input for RTD drinks, latte mixes, bakery and confectionery. FAQ

Common questions.

What EGCG, caffeine and color specs do beverage buyers request for matcha extract?
Beverage buyers typically specify EGCG and caffeine levels alongside color intensity, since the extract doubles as a natural colorant. Shade-grown leaf material generally gives a brighter green. These are commonly requested spec grades; actual specs, including color parameters, are confirmed on inquiry against your target profile.
When should buyers choose matcha extract over ground matcha leaf powder?
Ground matcha is whole-leaf powder and can leave sediment in drinks, while extract is concentrated for flavor and color with better solubility in beverage systems. Buyers formulating RTD or instant drinks usually prefer extract grades; those wanting whole-leaf character for premium positioning may specify leaf powder.
