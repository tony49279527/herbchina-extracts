FAQ

Common questions.

How is cordifolioside content measured in guduchi extract?
Cordifoliosides are diterpene glycosides, and specifications typically reference a standardized percentage analyzed by HPLC. Because the marker family includes several related compounds, buyers should clarify whether the specification covers a single cordifolioside or a group. We confirm the feasible range only after seeing your target spec.
Is the extract made from stem only, and does source material affect the marker?
Guduchi extract for supplement use is normally produced from the stem, but some supply chains draw on other plant parts, which can change the glycoside profile and analytical result. If stem-only sourcing is a requirement, note it in your inquiry so it can be verified with partner producers.
Can you provide documentation for a guduchi grade before purchase?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. Share the marker and method you need and we will align documentation to it.
