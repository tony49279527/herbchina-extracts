FAQ

Common questions.

What is the source material for your Solomon's seal extract?
Our Solomon's seal extract is derived from Polygonatum sibiricum rhizome. Because rhizome age and processing affect polysaccharide levels, we recommend confirming plant part and source references on the COA against your target specification.
How are polysaccharides measured in Solomon's seal extract?
Polysaccharide content is commonly measured by UV spectrophotometry after extraction and colorimetric reaction. Because reported values vary with method and reference standard, we align the agreed method with your target specification. COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec.
Can Solomon's seal extract be sourced to a custom polysaccharide grade?
Yes — tell us your target polysaccharide content, test method and intended product format, and we will confirm feasibility through partner producers before issuing a matched quotation.
