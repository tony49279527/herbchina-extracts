Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Soursop extract is derived from Annona muricata leaf and fruit, a tropical botanical widely sourced for functional wellness and beverage concepts. Buyers most often request grades defined by annonaceous acetogenin profile, with ratio-type specifications also in circulation. Demand has grown alongside botanical blend and functional tea development. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through vetted partner producers; buyers share their target specification and we confirm feasibility honestly before quoting.

FAQ

Common questions.

Which plant parts do you source soursop extract from, and does that change the specification?
Material is sourced from Annona muricata leaf and fruit depending on the buyer's target specification. Leaf-sourced and fruit-sourced extracts are treated as separate specification lines, since marker profile and matrix differ. Tell us the plant part referenced in your target spec and we will confirm feasibility with our partner producers.
How are annonaceous acetogenin profile grades typically defined for soursop extract?
Acetogenin grades in the market are generally described by total acetogenin content and, in some cases, by individual annonacin-type markers, with HPLC-based methods commonly referenced. Specifications vary between suppliers, so the testing scope and calculation basis should be agreed in writing. We match your stated profile against what our partner producers can realistically offer.
Can a COA and TDS be provided for soursop extract?
A COA and TDS can be arranged on request; testing scope is agreed per buyer specification so the documents reflect the parameters you actually require. Share your target marker, method preference and any limits, and we will confirm what documentation the producer can support before order.
