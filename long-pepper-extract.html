Long Pepper Extract

Long pepper — pippali fruit extract for bioavailability-enhancer formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Long pepper extract is produced from the fruit of Piper longum, known in Ayurvedic tradition as pippali and widely requested as a piperine-standardized input for bioavailability-enhancer formulations. Supplement and nutraceutical buyers use it alongside curcumin, CoQ10 and other actives. As a Xi'an trading company working through partner producers, we confirm piperine grade, method and documentation once your target spec is received.

Specifications below reflect commonly requested options in the market — not a stock list. Share your target spec and we'll confirm exactly what we can supply, honestly.

FAQ

Common questions.

What piperine grades of long pepper extract do buyers typically request?
The most commonly requested grade is 95% piperine, typically tested by HPLC, with lower standardized grades also traded depending on cost and formulation targets. Actual specification and method are confirmed on inquiry against your target spec.
Is your long pepper extract sourced from the fruit of Piper longum rather than Piper nigrum?
Yes, material is sourced from Piper longum fruit (pippali). Because piperine is also present in black pepper, buyers sometimes request source verification; we confirm the applicable identity and marker testing scope with partner producers.
Can you supply a COA and TDS aligned to our piperine specification?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. Please share your required piperine method, limits and any additional parameters so documents can be aligned before quoting.
