What it is, and how we handle it.

FAQ

Common questions.

What cuminaldehyde levels are commonly requested, and how is the marker analyzed?
Cuminaldehyde is the marker usually referenced, with buyers requesting different standardized percentages depending on the application. GC is the method most often cited for volatile marker analysis. Share your target level and method so feasibility can be reviewed.
Is the extract derived from seed only, and can carrier or extract ratio be specified?
Cuminum cyminum seed is the standard starting material. Buyers sometimes specify a carrier or a particular extract ratio for powder handling; include any such requirements in your inquiry so they can be assessed with our partner producers.
How do we request COA and TDS against our specification?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. Send your parameter list and acceptance criteria and we will coordinate documentation through our supply partners.
