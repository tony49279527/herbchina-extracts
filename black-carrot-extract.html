Black Carrot Extract

Black Carrot — a clean-label anthocyanin colorant for food and beverage lines.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Black carrot extract comes from the root of dark purple Daucus carota cultivars and is one of the most widely used natural red-to-purple colorants. Buyers typically specify anthocyanin content and color strength. Demand tracks clean-label reformulation in beverages, confectionery and dairy alternatives. We are a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers against buyer-declared color and marker targets.

FAQ

Common questions.

How is anthocyanin content measured in black carrot extract?
Anthocyanin content is commonly determined by UV-Vis spectrophotometry, with HPLC used when buyers need individual anthocyanin profiles. Color strength may also be expressed against a standard. Buyers usually state the method alongside the target value so results remain comparable across lots.
What is the difference between black carrot color grades and standardized anthocyanin grades?
Color grades are typically defined by coloring strength and shade performance in application, while standardized grades declare a specific anthocyanin percentage. Buyers reformulating a beverage often work to a color strength target, whereas supplement buyers tend to work to a declared anthocyanin figure. We quote against whichever dimension your specification uses.
Can you provide documentation for a black carrot extract spec review?
COA and TDS can be arranged on request; testing scope agreed per buyer specification. Share your target anthocyanin or color strength, preferred carrier and application, and we will confirm what our vetted partner producers can realistically offer.
