Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Spermidine is a naturally occurring polyamine found at notable levels in wheat germ, and wheat germ extract has become a common botanical source for spermidine-standardized powders. Demand has grown alongside cellular-health and healthy-aging supplement categories, particularly in capsule and powder formats. We source through vetted partner producers and work to the spermidine percentage, source material and testing scope each buyer specifies.

FAQ

Common questions.

What spermidine content grades are commonly requested, and how is spermidine measured?
Wheat germ-derived spermidine powder is commonly requested at 0.5-1% spermidine. Quantitation is typically done by HPLC, though some suppliers use different chromatographic approaches, so buyers should confirm the method against their own acceptance criteria before agreeing a specification.
Is the spermidine synthetic or plant-derived?
This product is positioned as wheat germ (Triticum aestivum)-derived, not synthetic. Because source material and extract ratio affect labeling and marketing claims, buyers should state their source-material requirement in the inquiry so we can confirm what a partner producer can actually supply.
How do we arrange documentation for a spermidine spec?
Documentation can be arranged on request, with the testing scope agreed per buyer specification. Let us know the target spermidine percentage, assay method and any limits you require, and we will confirm feasibility before proceeding.
