Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Calendula extract is derived from the flower of Calendula officinalis, a well-known botanical in skincare and cosmetic development. Buyers request it in total-flavonoid and carotenoid grades for use in creams, serums and other topical formats. As a trading company based in Xi'an, we source through partner producers and confirm feasibility after receiving your target specification.

FAQ

Common questions.

What total-flavonoid or carotenoid grades are commonly requested for calendula extract?
Both total-flavonoid and carotenoid-standardized grades are commonly requested, with concentrations varying by buyer and cosmetic application. We confirm the feasible range against your target spec on inquiry.
Which part of the calendula plant is extracted and what solvents are typical?
The flower of Calendula officinalis is the source material. Extraction solvents vary, with water, ethanol and glycerin-based routes all traded. We confirm the extraction route against your requirement.
How do you handle COA and TDS for calendula extract?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. Send your required parameters and we will confirm what can be documented before sourcing.
