Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Creatine monohydrate (CAS 6020-87-7) remains one of the highest-volume actives in sports nutrition, used in powders, capsules and ready-to-mix formats. Buyers commonly request 99.5%+ by HPLC and often specify micronized grades around 200 mesh for improved dispersion and mouthfeel. As a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers, we align on your mesh, purity and packaging requirements and confirm feasibility honestly before you commit.

FAQ

Common questions.

What does micronized 200 mesh mean for my formulation?
Micronized grades are milled to a finer particle size, commonly around 200 mesh, which buyers often choose to improve dispersion, reduce settling and smooth mouthfeel in ready-to-mix powders. Standard non-micronized grades remain available if your process does not require it.
Which test method is used for the 99.5%+ purity claim?
HPLC is the commonly requested assay method for creatine monohydrate at 99.5%+. COA documentation can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification.
Can you match a specific mesh size or bulk density target?
Send your mesh, bulk density and purity targets and we will check what partner producers can realistically offer, then confirm honestly rather than overpromise.
