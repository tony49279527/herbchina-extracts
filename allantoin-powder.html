Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Allantoin is a cosmetic active with a long history in skin-conditioning-positioned personal care, valued for its compatibility across emulsion and aqueous systems. The 99% grade is the most commonly requested specification, typically assessed by HPLC or titration. Demand spans facial care, body care and oral care. FAQ

Common questions.

What purity grades of allantoin are commonly requested, and how is the content verified?
The 99% grade is the most commonly requested for cosmetic use. Content is typically verified by HPLC or titrimetric methods, with the specific method agreed against your target specification. Actual grades are confirmed on inquiry.
Is the allantoin you source synthetic or plant-derived, and does this affect formulation?
Allantoin traded for cosmetics is predominantly produced synthetically, which gives consistent purity; plant-derived material also exists. The choice usually depends on your marketing positioning and purity target, and we confirm the source route against the spec you require.
What documentation is available, and how do I request a custom specification?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. For custom grades, we review your parameters with partner producers and confirm feasibility after receiving your requirements.
