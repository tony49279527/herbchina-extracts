Pearl Powder

Hyriopsis cumingii — fine pearl powder for beauty and cosmetic formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Pearl powder is produced from the shell nacre of Hyriopsis cumingii and is composed mainly of calcium carbonate together with a matrix of amino acids and trace elements. It is traded in fine mesh sizes for cosmetic and, in some markets, food-adjacent beauty concepts. As a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers, we work from the buyer's target mesh and grade and confirm feasibility honestly.

Specifications below reflect commonly requested options in the market — not a stock list. Share your target spec and we'll confirm exactly what we can supply, honestly.

FAQ

Common questions.

What mesh sizes of pearl powder are commonly requested for cosmetic use?
Cosmetic buyers typically specify fine mesh sizes, with the finest grades used in pressed and loose powders and coarser grades in soap and mask formats. The exact mesh and distribution are agreed per buyer specification, and we confirm which mesh the partner producer can offer.
What is the typical composition of your pearl powder, and can amino acid content be specified?
Pearl powder is predominantly calcium carbonate with an amino acid matrix and trace elements. Buyers focused on the amino acid profile can request relevant tests within the agreed scope. Because composition varies with source and processing, the final specification is agreed per buyer specification.
What do you need to arrange COA/TDS and check feasibility for a pearl powder spec?
Share your target mesh size, color and whiteness expectations, heavy-metal limits and any microbial requirements. COA and TDS can be arranged on request; testing scope is agreed per buyer specification. We then check feasibility with the vetted partner producer honestly.
