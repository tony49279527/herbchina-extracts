FAQ

Common questions.

How is anthocyanin content normally expressed?
It is usually reported either as total anthocyanins by a UV/spectrophotometric method or as individual anthocyanins such as cyanidin-3-glucoside by HPLC. Buyers should state which convention their label or dossier expects; testing scope is confirmed against your target spec.
What carrier is used, and can it affect the anthocyanin reading?
Spray-dried berry powders commonly use maltodextrin or similar carriers, and the carrier ratio directly dilutes the apparent anthocyanin content. We disclose the carrier system used for any offer so the numbers can be compared on a like-for-like basis.
How should anthocyanin-rich powder be stored and what about color drift?
Anthocyanins are sensitive to heat, light and pH, so storage conditions are relevant to your specification discussion. Tell us your intended pH range and packaging assumptions and we will confirm what can be supported; COA/TDS are available on request.
