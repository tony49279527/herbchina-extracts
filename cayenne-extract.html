Cayenne Extract

Capsicum annuum fruit extract for metabolic-positioned and food flavor formulation programs.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Cayenne extract is produced from Capsicum annuum fruit and is positioned around capsaicinoid content, most commonly capsaicin. Buyers request it for metabolic-positioned formulations and for food flavor work where heat level matters. As a Xi'an trading company we source through partner producers; grade feasibility and documentation are confirmed after we receive your target spec.

FAQ

Common questions.

How is capsaicin content normally expressed and tested in cayenne extract?
Buyers usually specify total capsaicinoids or capsaicin as a percentage, often alongside Scoville heat units. Testing is typically HPLC-based. These are commonly requested spec grades; actual specs are confirmed on inquiry.
Which plant part and extraction route do you work with?
The material comes from Capsicum annuum fruit, with solvent extraction routes commonly discussed. We confirm the declared plant part and extraction method against partner producer feasibility for your target spec.
What should I send to start a cayenne extract inquiry?
Send your target capsaicin or capsaicinoid grade, intended application and required documentation. COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your buyer's target spec.
