Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Fenugreek seed extract has moved into the mainstream of men's vitality and metabolic-positioning categories, driven by markers such as saponins and 4-hydroxyisoleucine. It is widely used in testosterone-support and glucose-metabolism-positioned blends. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through vetted partner producers and confirm feasibility against each buyer's target specification rather than promising fixed stock.

FAQ

Common questions.

Which marker should I request for a men's vitality product?
Saponins are the most commonly requested marker for vitality-positioning products, often with 4-hydroxyisoleucine specified for metabolic angles. The right choice depends on your formulation narrative and label claims.
Are both saponin-standardised and 4-hydroxyisoleucine-standardised specs available?
Yes, both are traded in the market. Share your preferred marker level and testing method and we will confirm feasibility through our partner producers.
How do you handle specification changes?
Buyers often refine their target spec during development. We review the revised requirements and confirm honestly what can be arranged, without overcommitting on supply.
