Documentation

Paperwork your regulatory team can review.

We don't claim in-house labs or certifications we don't hold. COA and TDS documents can be arranged on request, and testing scope is agreed against your specification before supply. Cosmetic buyers typically ask for a COA per batch, a specification sheet, and an MSDS — we arrange these on request and answer the questions your regulatory team raises.

FAQ

Common questions.

What is your minimum order quantity for cosmetic ingredients?
MOQs are flexible and depend on the material and packaging. Share the ingredient and target volume and we will confirm what is feasible.
Can I request samples for formulation trials?
Yes. Samples can be arranged on request. Tell us the material and the quantity needed for your trial.
What documentation do you provide for cosmetic regulatory review?
COA/TDS documents can be arranged on request, along with available specification sheets. Requirements vary by market, so review the documents against your own framework. We don't claim our documentation satisfies any specific regulatory framework.
Can you customize a specification for our formulation?
In some cases, yes. Share the target specification and application and we will confirm feasibility with our partner producers.
Do you supply both supplement and cosmetic grades?
We handle botanical extracts across supplement, cosmetic, and food applications. Grade and specification are agreed per material and per customer requirement.
