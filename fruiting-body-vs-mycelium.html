Fruiting Body vs Mycelium Extract

The single most important distinction in mushroom extracts — and the one most labels hope you won't ask about.

HerbChina Extracts · Resources

Two products can both be called "reishi extract" while containing almost nothing in common. The difference is what part of the organism was extracted — and most buyers never think to ask.

The three things sold as "mushroom extract"

  • Fruiting body extract — extracted from the actual mushroom (the cap and stem). This is the traditional medicinal material, and where beta-glucans and triterpenoids are concentrated.
  • Pure mycelium extract — extracted from the root-like mycelial network, grown in liquid culture without grain. A legitimate material with its own profile, but different from fruiting body.
  • Mycelium on grain — mycelium grown on rice, oats, or sorghum, then ground up with the grain still in it. The result can be mostly starch, with a mushroom label on the front.

Why beta-glucan is the marker that matters

Beta-glucans are the best-studied active compounds in functional mushrooms. A serious supplier states the beta-glucan percentage on the spec sheet. What many don't volunteer is the alpha-glucan (starch) content alongside it — high alpha-glucan is the fingerprint of grain filler.

Rule of thumb: if a "mushroom extract" lists polysaccharides but won't break out beta-glucan separately, treat the number with suspicion. Polysaccharide tests count starch too.

Four questions to ask any supplier

  1. What was extracted — fruiting body, mycelium, or mycelium on grain? The answer should be one thing, stated plainly, not "a proprietary blend."
  2. What is the beta-glucan content, and the alpha-glucan content? Both numbers. If they only know one, they haven't tested properly.
  3. What test method was used? Beta-glucan should be measured by a recognized method — ask which one.
  4. Can I see a batch-specific COA? With the species' Latin name, the source material declared, and the glucan numbers printed on it.
Our position: we declare fruiting body vs. mycelium source on every mushroom product, state beta-glucan content per product TDS, and provide batch documentation on request. No blending games.
