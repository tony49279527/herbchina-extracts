Papain Powder

Papain (Carica papaya) — a papaya latex-derived proteolytic enzyme powder for digestive-enzyme positioned formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Papain is a proteolytic enzyme obtained from the latex of papaya (Carica papaya), widely used in digestive-enzyme supplements and multi-enzyme blends. Activity is conventionally expressed in USP units, and buyers frequently specify both the activity level and the physical form they need. We source through vetted partner producers and confirm activity, carrier and testing scope against each buyer's stated specification.

FAQ

Common questions.

How is papain activity measured, and what units do buyers typically specify?
Papain activity is conventionally expressed in USP units, based on a defined proteolytic assay. Because different assay conditions yield different values, buyers should state the activity level and the exact method their QC team will use so both sides are working from the same basis.
What is the source material for papain, and does form matter?
Papain is sourced from the latex of the papaya plant (Carica papaya). It has historically been traded in different physical forms, so buyers should specify the form they need and the activity level, and we will confirm what a partner producer can support for that specification.
How do we confirm papain activity against our own spec?
A COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. Provide your target USP activity, assay method and any carrier requirements, and we will confirm feasibility honestly.
