FAQ

Common questions.

How is natural vitamin C measured, and does the method affect the declared figure?
Natural vitamin C is commonly determined by titration or by HPLC. Because ascorbic acid is sensitive to heat and oxygen, results can shift with processing and storage, and buyers sometimes request both a measured content and a stability indication. The method and reporting basis are agreed per buyer specification.
What plant part is used, and how do buyers distinguish whole-fruit material from fortified blends?
Material is sourced from the Myrciaria dubia fruit. Adulteration checkpoints buyers raise include fortification with synthetic ascorbic acid, blending with cheaper fruit sources and undeclared carrier dilution. These can be explored through marker ratios and additional testing, with the specific checks agreed per buyer specification.
Can you provide documentation for my clean-label review?
A COA/TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. Share your target vitamin C level, fruit-part requirement and required test panel, and we will confirm feasibility honestly through our vetted partner producers.
