Rocket leaf — emerging botanical extract for nutricosmetic and beauty-from-within formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Rocket leaf extract is derived from the leaf of Eruca sativa and has emerged as an ingredient of interest in nutricosmetics and hair-health oriented formulations, where it is positioned around glucosinolate content. Buyers in the beauty-from-within space often request it for capsule and powder formats alongside other botanical actives. Specification interest centers on glucosinolate level and the analytical method used to report it. We source through vetted partner producers and confirm feasibility honestly before you commit.

FAQ

Common questions.

How are glucosinolates typically measured in rocket leaf extract?
Glucosinolates are commonly determined by HPLC, sometimes after enzymatic conversion to related breakdown products, and results depend heavily on the reference standard and whether total or individual glucosinolates are reported. Because the numbers are method-sensitive, agree the analytical approach with your lab before comparing offers. Include the specific glucosinolate or total figure your formulation targets.
Which part of the plant is used, and does harvest timing matter?
The extract is produced from the leaf of Eruca sativa, and glucosinolate levels are known to vary with plant age, growing conditions and harvest timing, so raw material variability is a real consideration. Producers aiming at consistent marker levels typically control harvest windows and blend lots. Ask for the plant part and harvest basis if consistency is critical to your product.
What supporting documents can be arranged for a rocket leaf specification?
COA and TDS can be arranged on request; testing scope is agreed per buyer specification. Share your target glucosinolate level, method preference and any claim-related testing needs, and we will confirm what the partner producer can support.
