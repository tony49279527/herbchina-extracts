Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Maitake extract is a mushroom extract standardized from the fruiting body of Grifola frondosa, used by supplement brands in immune-support, metabolic, and everyday wellness formulations. Buyers sourcing maitake usually want a clearly declared source material and a polysaccharide or beta-glucan marker that matches their label. As a Xi'an-based trading company founded in 2019, we work with partner producers and confirm what we can supply against your target spec.

Specifications below reflect commonly requested options in the market — not a stock list. Share your target spec and we'll confirm exactly what we can supply, honestly.
