Rhodiola Rosea Extract

Rhodiola rosea — Root extract standardized to rosavins and salidroside — for energy, focus, and adaptogen product lines.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Rhodiola rosea extract is standardized from the root and typically measured on two markers: rosavins and salidroside. It's a staple in energy, cognitive, and adaptogen formulas. As a trading company, we work with partner producers and confirm supply against the spec you actually need.

Specifications below reflect commonly requested options in the market — not a stock list. Share your target spec and we'll confirm exactly what we can supply, honestly.
