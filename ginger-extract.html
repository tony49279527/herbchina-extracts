Ginger Extract

Zingiber officinale — a digestive staple standardised to gingerols for supplement, food and beverage formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Ginger extract is a versatile digestive-positioning botanical, standardised to gingerols and used across supplements, foods, beverages and confectionery. It is a stable, high-volume trade item with gingerols 5% being the most commonly requested specification. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through vetted partner producers and align each enquiry to realistic market options.

Specifications below reflect commonly requested options in the market — not a stock list. Share your target spec and we'll confirm exactly what we can supply, honestly.

FAQ

Common questions.

What gingerol specifications are typically available?
5% gingerols by HPLC is the most commonly traded specification, with higher concentrations also requested for concentrated formulas. Share your target and we will confirm feasibility.
Is the extract suitable for food and beverage applications?
Ginger extract is widely used in food and beverage formats. Suitability depends on your carrier, solubility and flavour requirements; we can review these with you before confirming a specification.
How do you handle quality documentation?
A COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. We confirm what analytical data can be provided prior to order.
