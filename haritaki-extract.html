Haritaki — Terminalia chebula fruit extract for digestive-positioned formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Haritaki extract is derived from the fruit of Terminalia chebula, a core Ayurvedic material traditionally positioned around digestive wellness and valued for its tannin content. Supplement and functional-food buyers request it on tannin or chebulagic acid grades. As a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through partner producers, we confirm marker grade, test method and documentation against the buyer's target specification.

FAQ

Common questions.

Which marker grades of Haritaki extract are commonly requested?
Buyers commonly request tannin-standardized grades in the 30% to 60% range, and in some cases chebulagic acid grades, depending on positioning and cost targets. These are reference ranges only; actual specification is confirmed on inquiry.
How is the tannin content in Terminalia chebula extract tested?
Tannin content is typically assessed by UV or titrimetric methods, while individual compounds such as chebulagic acid may be tested by HPLC where requested. We confirm the applicable method against your target spec with partner producers.
What documents can be provided with Haritaki extract?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. Share your marker, method and limit requirements so documentation can be aligned before order confirmation.
