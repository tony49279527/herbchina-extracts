Bromelain Powder

Bromelain (Ananas comosus) — a pineapple stem-derived enzyme powder for digestive-enzyme positioned formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Bromelain is a proteolytic enzyme preparation obtained from pineapple stem, with activity conventionally expressed in gelatin-digesting units (GDU). It is a long-established ingredient in digestive-enzyme and protein-digestion supplement ranges, often blended with other enzymes. We source through vetted partner producers and align activity, carrier and testing scope with the specification each buyer provides.

FAQ

Common questions.

What does GDU/g mean, and which bromelain activity grades are commonly traded?
GDU/g (gelatin-digesting units per gram) expresses bromelain's proteolytic activity. Commonly requested market grades fall between 2000 and 3000 GDU/g. Because activity assays differ, buyers should specify the assay method their QC team will apply when agreeing a specification.
Is the bromelain sourced from pineapple stem or fruit?
This product is positioned as pineapple stem (Ananas comosus)-derived, the conventional commercial source for higher-activity bromelain. Buyers with a specific plant-part or source-material requirement should state it in the inquiry so we can confirm what a partner producer can supply.
What does it take to lock in a bromelain activity spec?
A COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. Share your target GDU/g, assay method and any carrier or excipient preferences, and we will confirm feasibility before proceeding.
