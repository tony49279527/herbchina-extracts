Valerian Root Extract

Valeriana officinalis — a classic calm and sleep-positioning root extract for supplement and functional beverage formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Valerian root extract is one of the most recognised calm and sleep-positioning botanicals, standardised to valerenic acids. It remains a staple in sleep-support and relaxation blends across US and EU markets, frequently paired with hops, lemon balm or chamomile. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through vetted partner producers and align each enquiry to a realistic, widely-traded specification.

Specifications below reflect commonly requested options in the market — not a stock list. Share your target spec and we'll confirm exactly what we can supply, honestly.

FAQ

Common questions.

What valerenic acid levels are typically requested?
0.8% valerenic acids by HPLC is the most commonly traded specification. Some buyers request lower or higher levels depending on the blend; share your target and we will confirm what our partner producers can realistically supply.
Is a 4:1 or 5:1 ratio extract available?
Ratio extracts are also traded in the market. Whether a ratio or a standardised marker spec is right depends on your formulation and labelling goals; we can review both options with you.
How do you handle quality documentation?
A COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. We confirm what analytical data can be provided before quotation.
