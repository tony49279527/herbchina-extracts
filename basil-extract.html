FAQ

Common questions.

How is ursolic acid in basil extract typically tested?
Ursolic acid is generally quantified by HPLC against a reference standard, and the result is reported as a percentage of the extract. Because the ratio extract and the ursolic-acid grade are separate products, buyers should choose the one that ties to their intended positioning.
What plant parts are used, and does the species matter?
The feedstock is normally the aerial parts of Ocimum basilicum, including leaf and stem. Species and cultivar can shift the phytochemical profile, and buyers occasionally specify other Ocimum species, so the exact botanical should be confirmed up front.
Can you supply basil extract to a custom spec and provide analysis paperwork?
Yes, share your target ratio or ursolic-acid percentage, particle size and packaging, and we will confirm what can realistically be delivered. COA and TDS can be arranged on request; testing scope is agreed per buyer specification.
