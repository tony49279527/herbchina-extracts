FAQ

Common questions.

Is guava leaf extract usually standardized to quercetin or to tannins?
Both approaches appear in the market. Some buyers specify quercetin content by HPLC, while others work around total tannin levels or a defined extract ratio. Because methods differ, we ask for your marker and test method before confirming a grade.
What extraction methods are common for guava leaf?
Water and water-ethanol extraction are the most widely used routes, followed by concentration and drying to powder. Solvent choice affects the polyphenol and tannin profile, so buyers should state any solvent restrictions in their target specification.
Does the leaf source region affect the specification?
Leaf origin and harvest timing can shift polyphenol and tannin levels. Partner producers may source from different regions depending on the grade requested. We confirm the proposed source and achievable marker range with you before quoting.
