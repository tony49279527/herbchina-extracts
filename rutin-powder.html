Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Rutin is a flavonoid glycoside obtained commercially from Sophora japonica flower bud, where it occurs at relatively high levels. It is a widely traded botanical ingredient in flavonoid and antioxidant-positioned supplement ranges, commonly supplied at high HPLC purity. We source through vetted partner producers and align purity, source material and testing scope with the specification each buyer provides.

FAQ

Common questions.

What rutin purity grades are commonly requested, and how is purity determined?
Rutin is commonly requested at 95%+ as determined by HPLC. Some suppliers also offer lower-percentage or extract-ratio grades, so buyers should specify the purity basis and the assay method to avoid mismatched expectations at QC.
Which plant part is the rutin sourced from?
This product is positioned as Sophora japonica flower bud-derived. Because source material and extract ratio affect labeling and formulation choices, buyers should state their plant-part preference in the inquiry so we can confirm what a partner producer can supply.
Can documentation be arranged for a specific rutin specification?
A COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. Share your target purity, assay method and any solvent-residue or heavy-metal limits, and we will confirm feasibility before quoting.
