Green Coffee Bean Extract

Coffea arabica — unroasted bean extract for metabolic and weight-management positioned formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Green Coffee Bean Extract is derived from unroasted Coffea arabica beans and standardized to chlorogenic acid, a widely traded marker in metabolic and weight-management concepts. It is used in capsules, powders, and functional beverages. As a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers, HerbChina Extracts helps overseas supplement, food, beverage, and cosmetics brands compare commonly requested market specifications. Buyers share their target spec and we confirm feasibility honestly before quotation.

What specifications are commonly requested?
Commonly requested options include chlorogenic acid at 45–50%. Buyers can share target marker, carrier, particle size, and testing expectations; we confirm feasibility with vetted partner producers.
How is chlorogenic acid tested in your Green Coffee Bean Extract, and can you confirm the 45 to 50% HPLC grade?
Green Coffee Bean Extract is standardized on chlorogenic acid, with 45% to 50% by HPLC being a commonly requested range.
