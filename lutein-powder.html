Lutein Powder

Marigold-derived lutein with zeaxanthin as a co-marker, positioned for eye-health formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Lutein powder is a carotenoid extracted from Tagetes erecta marigold, typically supplied with zeaxanthin as a naturally occurring co-marker. It anchors most eye-health ranges across supplement and functional food formats. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through vetted partner producers and confirm honestly what a given lutein or zeaxanthin specification can realistically support.

FAQ

Common questions.

What is the typical ratio of lutein to zeaxanthin in marigold-derived material?
Marigold oleoresin naturally delivers both, with lutein typically dominant, and some buyers request specific ratios or added zeaxanthin. The exact ratio is agreed per buyer specification.
Which lutein grades are commonly quoted, and what method is used?
Grades clustered around 5%, 10% and 20% are commonly requested, normally measured by HPLC. Water-dispersible variants exist for beverage use, and the final grade and method are agreed per buyer specification.
Can COA and TDS be arranged for a custom lutein or zeaxanthin target?
COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. Give us your target lutein and zeaxanthin levels and intended format and we will confirm feasibility with a partner producer.
