Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

White willow bark is a long-traded botanical in joint comfort and mobility supplement categories. Extract is standardized to salicin, with 15-25% commonly requested. We source through vetted partner producers and confirm each buyer's marker level, ratio and carrier requirements before providing a quotation.

FAQ

Common questions.

What salicin level do you typically see requested?
15-25% salicin is commonly requested in the market. Tell us your target specification and we will confirm feasibility with our partner producers.
Can the white willow bark COA confirm salicin by HPLC and note the source plant part used?
White willow bark extract is commonly standardized to 15-25% salicin by HPLC, and buyers often want the COA to verify both the salicin content and that the material is derived from bark rather than other plant parts. COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per your target specification. Feasibility is confirmed through partner producers after receiving your target spec.
Is a lower-salicin ratio extract available?
Ratio extracts are also commonly traded alongside standardized material. Advise your preference and we will respond honestly on supply.
