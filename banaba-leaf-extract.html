Banaba Leaf Extract

Lagerstroemia speciosa leaf extract standardized to corosolic acid, positioned for metabolic-support formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Banaba leaf extract is a botanical powder produced from the leaves of Lagerstroemia speciosa, almost always standardized to corosolic acid. It sits with bitter melon and similar actives in the metabolic-formulation toolkit. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through vetted partner producers and give buyers a straight read on what a given specification realistically supports.

FAQ

Common questions.

What corosolic acid grades are typically traded, and what method is used?
Low grades around 1-2% and higher grades clustering at 10%, 18% and 20% are commonly requested, with HPLC as the routine method. The exact grade and method are agreed per buyer specification.
Is the material leaf-only, or are twig and stem fractions present?
Authentic material is leaf-derived, but twig and stem fractions do appear in cheaper offers. If this matters to your specification, raise it up front so it can be addressed in the testing scope agreed with the producer.
Can COA and TDS be arranged for a custom corosolic acid level?
COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. Send your target corosolic acid content and method and we will confirm feasibility with a partner producer.
