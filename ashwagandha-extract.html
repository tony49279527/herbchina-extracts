FAQ

Common questions.

What withanolide specs can you supply?
Commonly requested options in the market are 2.5% and 5% withanolides by HPLC. Share your target spec and we'll confirm what we can supply.
Can you do a custom specification?
Yes — send us your target marker range, mesh, and any testing requirements, and we'll come back with what's feasible.
Do you provide a COA?
COA/TDS documents can be arranged on request. The testing scope is agreed against your specification so the documents match what you need.
How do I choose between 2.5% and 5% withanolides, and what should I check to avoid root or species substitution?
A 5% withanolide spec concentrates the marker and lowers the dose per capsule, while 2.5% gives more flexibility on cost and in blends, so pick based on your label claim and dosage form. Because ashwagandha is sometimes cut with other Withania material or non-root parts, ask for an identity check alongside the withanolide assay and specify the Withania somnifera root source, mesh and marker range, and we will confirm what partner producers can supply, with COA/TDS arranged on request.
