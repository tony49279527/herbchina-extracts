FAQ

Common questions.

What is the difference between shellfish-derived and fermentation glucosamine sulfate?
Shellfish-derived glucosamine is produced from chitin extracted from crustacean shells, while fermentation routes use microbial processes on plant-derived substrates. The choice usually comes down to label and allergen considerations in your target market. We confirm which route matches your requirement once we have your target spec.
How is glucosamine sulfate content typically stabilized and tested?
Because glucosamine sulfate is hygroscopic, it is often supplied stabilized with sodium chloride or potassium chloride, and buyers should clarify which form they want. Content is usually verified by HPLC or titration against the glucosamine base. Testing scope is confirmed against your target specification on inquiry.
Can you confirm whether a vegetarian grade is feasible for our order?
Vegetarian fermentation grades are commonly traded alongside shellfish-derived material, but feasibility depends on your target spec, volume and timing. Share your requirement and we will confirm what our partner producers can offer before any commitment.
