Evening primrose oil — GLA-bearing seed oil for cosmetic and supplement formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Evening primrose oil is a seed oil from Oenothera biennis, sought after for its gamma-linolenic acid (GLA) content. It is widely requested by cosmetic and supplement brands for formulations positioned around skin and women's wellness. What GLA content is commonly requested for evening primrose oil?
GLA (gamma-linolenic acid) content is the primary marker, with GLA grades commonly requested. Typical market grades range by percentage, and buyers often specify a minimum GLA level. These are commonly requested spec grades; actual specs are confirmed on inquiry against your target profile.
