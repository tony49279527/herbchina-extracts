Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Luteolin powder is a flavonoid standardized to high purity, most often sourced from peanut shell or Chrysanthemum. It has gained traction in capsule and powder-blend categories positioned around cellular antioxidant support and everyday wellness. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through vetted partner producers and confirm assay, source material and test method against each buyer's target spec before committing.

FAQ

Common questions.

What source materials are used for luteolin powder, and how does that affect the spec?
Luteolin is commonly produced from peanut shell or from Chrysanthemum. The two routes can differ in color, residual solvents profile and co-eluting flavonoids, so buyers with allergen-related concerns or a preferred botanical origin should state the source they want at inquiry stage.
Which grades and test methods are typically requested for luteolin?
The most frequently requested grade is 98% by HPLC, with 90% and 95% also traded. Some buyers additionally specify a maximum for related flavonoids such as apigenin or a limit on residual solvents, which we confirm with the producer against the agreed test method.
How do I request a quotation and supporting documentation for a custom luteolin spec?
Send your target purity, source preference, test method, particle size and packing format. We will check feasibility with vetted partner producers and reply honestly. A COA can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification.
