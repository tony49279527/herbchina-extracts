Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Manjistha extract is prepared from the root of Rubia cordifolia and is a familiar ingredient in skin-focused supplement and cosmetic development. Buyers often request grades standardized to purpurin, manjistin, or total quinone-type colorants, since these compounds are also linked to the extract's natural red hue. From our Xi'an trading base, we coordinate with partner producers and confirm sourcing feasibility after receiving a buyer's target specification.

FAQ

Common questions.

What is the difference between purpurin and manjistin specifications?
Both are quinone-type compounds found in Rubia cordifolia root, and either can serve as the marker depending on the buyer's convention. Purpurin is often chosen for its stronger color contribution, while manjistin specifications appear in more traditional herbal contexts. Some buyers combine them into a total content figure. HPLC is the usual analytical approach; tell us your convention and we will check feasibility.
Does manjistha extract always carry a strong red color?
The natural color depends on the quinone content and the concentration level of the finished powder. Higher-marker grades generally show more pronounced color, which matters for cosmetic applications where shade consistency is monitored. If color is a critical attribute, include a color or appearance parameter in your specification so it can be evaluated.
How do I request a COA or TDS for a manjistha grade?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. Provide your marker and method requirements and we will match them to available partner-producer documentation.
