Bibhitaki Extract

Bibhitaki — Triphala fruit botanical for traditional digestive formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Bibhitaki extract comes from the fruit of Terminalia bellirica, one of the three Triphala fruits, and is usually specified around tannin or gallic acid content. It is requested for traditional digestive-positioned formulas and Triphala-based blends. From Xi'an, we work as a trading company sourcing through partner producers, confirming grade feasibility after your target specification arrives.

FAQ

Common questions.

How are tannin and gallic acid grades typically specified and analyzed?
Buyers commonly reference total tannins or gallic acid percentages, and both UV-based and HPLC methods appear in market documentation depending on the parameter. Share your target marker level and preferred analytical method so the specification can be checked.
Is the extract made from the whole fruit, and how does Triphala positioning affect sourcing?
Terminalia bellirica fruit is the standard starting material for Bibhitaki extract. Because it is one of the three Triphala fruits, some buyers source it alongside the other two for blend consistency; mention if you need matched grades across a Triphala set.
What is the process for obtaining COA or TDS with our parameters?
COA and TDS are available on request, and testing scope is confirmed against your target spec. Provide your parameter list and acceptance criteria and we will coordinate documentation review with our supply partners.
