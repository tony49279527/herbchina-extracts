FAQ

Common questions.

Which mesh sizes of creatine monohydrate powder are commonly traded?
Mesh sizes from 80 to 200 are commonly requested, with finer mesh grades favored for ready-to-mix beverages and coarser grades for capsules or blends. Mesh preference should be stated in your target specification, and feasibility is confirmed with partner producers.
How is creatine monohydrate purity and identity verified?
Purity is often confirmed by HPLC or titration, with additional checks on moisture, heavy metals, microbial limits and identity. Some buyers also request a check for creatinine and dicyandiamide. Testing scope is confirmed against your target spec, and COA/TDS are available on request.
What documentation is available for creatine monohydrate before purchase?
COA and TDS are available on request, with the testing scope confirmed against your target specification. Documentation typically covers assay, mesh, moisture and microbiology, though the exact panel depends on your requirement.
