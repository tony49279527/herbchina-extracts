Buyer note

What to specify when you inquire.

A 5% withanolide spec concentrates the marker and lowers the dose per capsule, while 2.5% gives more flexibility on cost and in blends. Because ashwagandha is sometimes cut with other Withania material or non-root parts, specify the Withania somnifera root source, mesh, and marker range — and ask for an identity check alongside the withanolide assay.
