Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Buyers in fragrance and botanical-formulation categories often specify artemisinin orientation. FAQ

Common questions.

Which plant part of Artemisia annua does your extract use?
Our sweet wormwood extract is produced from Artemisia annua aerial parts, with a focus on leaf-derived material. Because artemisinin content concentrates in leaf, we recommend confirming the plant part and content basis on the COA against your target specification.
How is artemisinin content tested in sweet wormwood extract?
Artemisinin is commonly measured by HPLC with appropriate detection, and some buyers request reference-standard verification. Because the test approach affects reported values, we align the agreed method with your target spec. COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec.
Can you supply sweet wormwood extract to a specific artemisinin content?
Yes — provide your required artemisinin content, test method and intended application, and we will confirm feasibility through partner producers before quoting.
