Honeysuckle Extract

Honeysuckle — a chlorogenic-acid-standardized flower extract for immune-support and functional-beverage formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Honeysuckle extract is obtained from the flower of Lonicera japonica and standardized to chlorogenic acid alongside other naturally occurring phenolic compounds. It is frequently requested for immune-support positioned products and functional beverages, typically between 5% and 25% chlorogenic acid. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through vetted partner producers and confirm feasibility against buyer specifications.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is the chlorogenic acid in honeysuckle extract measured the same way as in green coffee or eucommia extracts?
The marker is the same compound, but sample matrices differ, so extraction and cleanup steps in the assay can vary between botanical sources. Buyers should specify the expected method and, where relevant, note the botanical source so we can confirm what partner producers can test and document.
Which flower part and harvest stage is used, and does it affect the chlorogenic acid level?
Material is sourced from Lonicera japonica flowers, typically harvested as buds or open flowers. Chlorogenic acid content can vary with harvest stage, so buyers with a tight grade target should state it clearly and we will confirm feasibility with vetted partner producers.
Can you provide COA and TDS documents for a chosen honeysuckle grade?
A COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with the testing scope agreed per buyer specification. We confirm the relevant documents once your target grade and assay method are set.
