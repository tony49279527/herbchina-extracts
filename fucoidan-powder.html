Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Fucoidan powder is a sulfated polysaccharide extracted from brown seaweed, used in marine-ingredient, wellness and cosmetic-positioned formulations. Content grades and molecular weight profiles vary by source species and extraction method. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source fucoidan through partner producers and confirm source species, content grade and documentation feasibility after receiving your target specification.

Which brown seaweed species are commonly used as the fucoidan source, and can the species be specified?
Common source species include Undaria pinnatifida, Laminaria and Fucus species. Buyers can specify the preferred source, and we confirm availability with partner producers on inquiry.

Commonly requested fucoidan content grades vary by application, and content is often assessed by method such as the respective assay specified by the buyer. Exact grade and test method are confirmed against your target spec on inquiry.
