Bulk pineapple extract powder in bromelain-activity or spray-dried fruit grades for digestive-positioned and flavoring formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Pineapple extract is derived from Ananas comosus fruit and is traded mainly in two directions: bromelain-activity standardized grades and spray-dried fruit powder grades. Supplement brands request it for digestive-positioned formats, while food and beverage buyers use spray-dried grades for flavor and nutrition. FAQ

Common questions.

What is the difference between bromelain-activity pineapple extract and spray-dried pineapple powder?
Bromelain-activity grades are standardized on enzyme activity, often expressed in GDU/g, and are typically used in digestive-positioned supplements. Spray-dried fruit powder grades focus on flavor, color and soluble solids rather than enzyme activity. Buyers usually specify which route they need, and specs are confirmed on inquiry.
How is bromelain activity in pineapple extract tested?
Activity is commonly measured by GDU or CDU assays, with supporting tests for moisture, microbial limits and heavy metals. Because methods vary by supplier and region, the testing scope is confirmed against your target spec, and COA/TDS are available on request.
Can the extract be supplied as a spray-dried powder for beverage use?
Yes, spray-dried fruit powder is one of the commonly requested forms for beverage and food applications. Send your target specification, including carrier preference and solubility requirements, and we will confirm sourcing feasibility with our partner producers.
