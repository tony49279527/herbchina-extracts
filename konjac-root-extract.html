Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Konjac root extract is derived from the tuber of Amorphophallus konjac and standardized on glucomannan, a soluble polysaccharide widely used in fiber-focused supplement formats. Demand has grown alongside interest in satiety-fiber capsules, powders, and functional food concepts. We are a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through vetted partner producers; buyers share their target glucomannan level and viscosity expectations, and we confirm feasibility and documentation honestly before any order.

FAQ

Common questions.

How is glucomannan content measured, and what grades are commonly requested?
Glucomannan is typically determined by HPLC or a validated spectrophotometric method, depending on the producer. 90%+ is the most commonly requested grade, with some buyers asking for higher. Viscosity is often specified alongside purity because it affects how the fiber performs in a formulation, so include it if it matters to you.
What is the difference between konjac glucomannan powder and konjac gum?
Both come from the same tuber, but they are processed and specified differently — glucomannan extract is purified toward a marker content, while konjac gum is generally positioned around its thickening and gelling behavior. Tell us which direction your formulation takes and we will confirm what the partner producer can supply.
Can documentation be provided for our quality review?
A COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with the testing scope agreed per buyer specification. Share your target glucomannan level, method, and any additional limits, and we will confirm what can be documented.
