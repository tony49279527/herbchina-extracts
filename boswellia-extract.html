Boswellia Extract

Boswellia serrata — a popular joint-comfort-positioning resin extract for capsule and tablet formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Boswellia serrata extract is a well-established joint-comfort-positioning botanical, standardised to boswellic acids and increasingly to AKBA. It features in joint, mobility and sports-recovery blends across US and EU markets. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through vetted partner producers and confirm each buyer's marker requirements against realistic market options.

FAQ

Common questions.

Should I specify total boswellic acids or AKBA?
Both are commonly requested. Total boswellic acids at 65% suits general joint-positioning products, while AKBA-standardised specs at 10–30% are often requested for premium positioning. We can review both with you.
Is boswellia often combined with other botanicals?
Yes, boswellia is frequently blended with curcumin, piperine and other joint-positioning botanicals. We can source multiple extracts so you can blend at your manufacturing site.
How is documentation handled?
A COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. We confirm the analytical package before quotation.
