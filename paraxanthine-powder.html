Paraxanthine Powder

Paraxanthine — high-purity caffeine metabolite for energy and sports-nutrition formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Paraxanthine (1,7-dimethylxanthine) is the primary human metabolite of caffeine, supplied here as a standalone powder for formulators building energy and sports-nutrition products. Buyers commonly request high-purity grades and defined assay values. As a Xi'an-based trading company we source through partner producers and confirm feasibility once your target specification is received.

FAQ

Common questions.

How is paraxanthine purity typically determined?
Paraxanthine assay is commonly determined by HPLC, with related xanthine impurities evaluated alongside the main peak. Testing scope is confirmed against your target spec, and a COA is available on request.
Is your paraxanthine isolated from natural sources or produced synthetically?
Paraxanthine supplied to formulators may be produced via different routes, and the appropriate one depends on your specification, labeling requirements and intended market. Share those details and we will confirm what our partner producers can offer before quoting.
Can you supply paraxanthine in a specific assay or particle size?
As a trading company sourcing through partner producers, we review custom assay, particle size and packaging requests per inquiry. Send your target specification and intended application, and we will confirm feasibility before quoting. TDS and COA are available on request.
