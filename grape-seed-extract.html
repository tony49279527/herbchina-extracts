Grape Seed Extract

Vitis vinifera — an antioxidant staple standardised to proanthocyanidins (OPC) for supplement and cosmetic formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Grape seed extract is a foundational antioxidant botanical, standardised to proanthocyanidins (OPC) and widely used across supplements, beverages and cosmetics. Demand is stable and global, with OPC 95% remaining the most traded specification. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through vetted partner producers and match enquiries to realistic market specifications.

Specifications below reflect commonly requested options in the market — not a stock list. Share your target spec and we'll confirm exactly what we can supply, honestly.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is OPC 95% the standard specification?
Yes, OPC 95% by UV-VIS is the most commonly traded specification. Lower concentrations such as 80% and 90% are also available in the market depending on your formulation and cost targets.
Can you supply both water-soluble and alcohol-soluble grades?
Solubility characteristics depend on the extraction and drying process used by the producer. Share your intended application and we will confirm what can realistically be arranged.
How is documentation handled?
A COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. We confirm the analytical package before quotation.
