What it is, and how we handle it.

FAQ

Common questions.

What saponin level is commonly traded?
40-90% saponins is commonly requested in the sports nutrition market. Tell us your target specification and we will confirm feasibility with partner producers.
Can protodioscin be specified?
Some buyers specify protodioscin levels rather than total saponins. Advise your preferred marker and we will respond honestly on what partner producers can test and supply.
Since saponins by UV are nonspecific, can the tribulus COA include protodioscin by HPLC for verification?
Tribulus extract is commonly traded at 40-90% saponins by UV, and because UV saponin values do not confirm the specific steroidal saponin profile, buyers often request protodioscin by HPLC as an additional marker. COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per your target specification. Feasibility of the HPLC testing is confirmed through partner producers after receiving your target spec.
