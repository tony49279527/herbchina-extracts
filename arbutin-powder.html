Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Alpha-arbutin is a cosmetic active based on a glucose-hydroquinone structure, widely used in skin-brightening-positioned skincare and colour cosmetics. The 99% grade is the most commonly requested specification, typically assessed by HPLC. Demand has grown alongside the clean-beauty and tone-evening segments. FAQ

Common questions.

What is the difference between alpha-arbutin and beta-arbutin, and which is more commonly requested?
Alpha-arbutin is the alpha-glucoside form and is generally more stable and more commonly requested by cosmetic formulators; beta-arbutin is the naturally occurring form. Buyers usually specify alpha-arbutin at 99% grade, and we confirm the exact form and assay against your target specification.
How is alpha-arbutin typically assayed, and what purity grades are realistic?
HPLC is the standard method for assaying alpha-arbutin content. The 99% grade is widely traded and is the most commonly requested, though lower grades exist for cost-sensitive projects. Actual specifications are confirmed on inquiry.
Do you provide COA and TDS, and can you match a custom cosmetic spec?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec. For custom requirements, we review your parameters with partner producers and confirm feasibility after receiving your detailed brief.
