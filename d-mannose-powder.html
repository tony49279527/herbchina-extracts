FAQ

Common questions.

What purity grades of D-Mannose are commonly traded, and how is the assay determined?
D-Mannose is most often traded at 99%+ assay in crystalline powder form, though lower-purity grades appear for certain applications. Assay is typically determined by HPLC. The precise test method and acceptance limits are agreed per buyer specification, and we confirm which purity the partner producer can offer.
Is your D-Mannose fermentation-derived, and can this be documented?
Yes, the grades we quote are fermentation-derived D-Mannose. Buyers who need confirmation of the production route can request it within the agreed testing scope. Documentation of origin is discussed case by case rather than assumed, and we confirm feasibility honestly before quoting.
What spec information do you need to arrange COA/TDS and confirm feasibility?
Send your target assay, particle size, moisture limits and any additional purity tests. COA and TDS can be arranged on request; testing scope is agreed per buyer specification. We then check the request against the vetted partner producer and respond honestly.
