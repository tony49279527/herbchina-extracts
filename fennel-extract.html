Fennel Extract

Foeniculum vulgare seed extract for digestive-positioned and flavor formulation programs.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Fennel extract is produced from Foeniculum vulgare seed and is positioned around volatile oil constituents, most commonly anethole and fenchone. Buyers request it for digestive-positioned formulations and for flavor and aroma work. As a Xi'an-based trading company sourcing through partner producers, we confirm grade feasibility and documentation only after receiving your target spec.

Specifications below reflect commonly requested options in the market — not a stock list. Share your target spec and we'll confirm exactly what we can supply, honestly.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do buyers usually specify anethole or total volatile oil for fennel extract?
Both are common. Some buyers request trans-anethole as a percentage, others ask for total volatile oil content with fenchone noted. These are commonly requested spec grades only; actual specs are confirmed on inquiry.
Is the extract made from seed or another plant part?
The standard material is Foeniculum vulgare seed. If your formulation requires a different plant part, we check feasibility with partner producers once we have your target spec.
How do I request COA and TDS for fennel extract?
COA and TDS are available on request, and the testing scope is confirmed against your buyer's target spec rather than a generic panel.
