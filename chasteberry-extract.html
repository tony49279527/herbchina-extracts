What it is, and how we handle it.

FAQ

Common questions.

What agnuside specifications are typically available?
0.5% agnuside by HPLC is the most commonly requested specification. Other levels may be available depending on the producer and your target; share your spec and we will confirm feasibility.
Is chasteberry often combined with other women's health botanicals?
Yes, chasteberry is frequently blended with other botanicals for women's health positioning. We can source multiple extracts so you can blend at your manufacturing site.
I need Low Agnuside Chasteberry Extract with a defined agnuside ceiling as well as a 0.5% minimum - can that be supported?
Agnuside is typically assayed by HPLC, and a defined upper limit can be built into the specification alongside the 0.5% minimum where the partner producer can meet it. A COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. We confirm what analytical data can be provided before order, including whether the chromatogram supports both the minimum and maximum limits. Feasibility is confirmed through our partner producers after receiving your target specification.
