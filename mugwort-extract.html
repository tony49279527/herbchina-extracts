Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Mugwort extract is prepared from Artemisia argyi leaf and is commonly standardized around its flavonoid content. Buyers working in traditional-wellness, herbal and topical product categories typically request flavonoid-oriented grades. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source mugwort extract through partner producers and confirm grading, documentation and feasibility only after receiving your target spec.

FAQ

Common questions.

Which species and plant part is your mugwort extract sourced from?
Our mugwort extract is sourced from Artemisia argyi leaf. Because Artemisia species can be visually similar, we recommend a species-confirmation and plant-part check on the COA against your target specification.
How are flavonoids tested in mugwort extract?
Total flavonoids are commonly reported by UV spectrophotometry, and individual flavone markers can be tested by HPLC when buyers need more specificity. Because method affects values, we confirm the agreed test approach against your target spec. COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec.
Can you supply mugwort extract with a custom flavonoid content?
Yes — share your required flavonoid content, preferred test method and application, and we will confirm feasibility through partner producers before quoting.
