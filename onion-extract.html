FAQ

Common questions.

How is quercetin quantified in onion extract, and are quercetin glycosides included in the value?
Quercetin is commonly determined by HPLC, and reporting may cover aglycone only or include glycoside forms after hydrolysis. Clarify which basis you need so testing scope can be confirmed against your target spec.
Is the extract derived from whole bulb, and how is total sulfur measured?
Material is derived from Allium cepa bulb, and total sulfur is typically assessed by a colorimetric or titrimetric method reflecting sulfur-containing compounds. Provide your required basis and range in your inquiry for confirmation.
What documents can you supply for an onion extract matched to our quercetin grade?
COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target specification. Share your quercetin or total-sulfur target and we will respond on documentation.
