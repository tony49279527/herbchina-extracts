What it is, and how we handle it.

FAQ

Common questions.

What source material and plant part is your zeaxanthin derived from?
Most commercially traded zeaxanthin is extracted from marigold (Tagetes erecta) flower petals, where it occurs alongside lutein esters. Fermentation-derived zeaxanthin is also available in the market for buyers who prefer a non-plant route. Which route suits your formulation depends on your label position and target spec, so we confirm the source before quoting.
How is zeaxanthin content typically tested, and what adulteration checks matter?
Marker content is usually verified by HPLC against a zeaxanthin reference standard. Because zeaxanthin and lutein are closely related xanthophylls, buyers often want the ratio between the two confirmed rather than a single total carotenoid figure. Testing scope is confirmed against your target spec when you inquire.
Can you supply a grade matched to our required zeaxanthin percentage?
Commonly requested grades sit at 5%, 10% and 20% zeaxanthin, though the exact figure depends on your finished-dose design. Share your target spec and intended format and we will confirm what is feasible through our partner producers before any commitment.
