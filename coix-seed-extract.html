Coix Seed Extract

Coix Seed — a classic East Asian botanical offered as ratio extract powder for beauty-from-within and botanical-wellness formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Coix Seed Extract is a water- or alcohol-extracted powder from the seed of Coix lacryma-jobi, a cereal grain long used in East Asian food and botanical traditions. Buyers commonly request 10:1 and 20:1 ratio grades for capsule, powder-blend and beverage concepts. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through vetted partner producers and confirm feasibility against your target specification honestly.

FAQ

Common questions.

What plant part and extraction method are used for Coix Seed Extract?
The starting material is the mature seed of Coix lacryma-jobi. Ratio extracts are typically produced by water or aqueous-alcohol extraction followed by concentration and spray drying. Buyers can specify the extraction solvent and carrier they prefer, and we confirm with the partner producer whether it can be matched.
How are 10:1 and 20:1 ratio grades verified?
Ratio extracts are generally controlled by input-to-output mass balance during production rather than by a single marker compound. Buyers often request supporting documentation such as extraction records or a representative COA. Testing scope is agreed per buyer specification, and a COA can be arranged on request.
Can you supply a custom ratio or specification for Coix Seed Extract?
Yes. Share your target ratio, carrier preference, particle size, and any microbiological or heavy-metal limits. We pass the specification to vetted partner producers and confirm feasibility honestly before you commit to anything.
