FAQ

Common questions.

What grade types are available for lavender extract, and how do buyers choose between them?
Two broad types are traded: ratio extracts (commonly 4:1 to 10:1) and standardized extracts built on markers such as luteolin or rosmarinic acid. Some buyers also ask for essential-oil-bearing versions. The right choice depends on whether the label calls for a ratio, a marker, or a characteristic lavender aroma.
Does the plant part or processing route affect the color and aroma of lavender extract powder?
The extract is made from Lavandula angustifolia flower. Water, ethanol-water and supercritical routes give different aroma intensity, color and solubility. Where a product's sensory profile matters, we recommend discussing the extraction route rather than relying on the product name alone.
Can you match a specific lavender extract spec and supply a COA?
Share your target ratio or marker, extraction route preference, particle size, carrier and packing format, and we will confirm feasibility with vetted partner producers. A COA can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification.
