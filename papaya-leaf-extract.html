FAQ

Common questions.

What does 'papain-adjacent enzyme grade' mean for papaya leaf extract?
Papaya leaf is not the primary commercial source of papain — that is the fruit latex — but buyers sometimes request leaf extracts specified around enzyme-related activity or flavonoid content. We ask you to define the marker and test method so the partner producer can confirm what is achievable.
Which part of the papaya plant is extracted, and what carrier options exist?
Commercial material is based on Carica papaya leaf. Powders are typically spray-dried or drum-dried, and buyers occasionally specify a carrier such as maltodextrin for standardization. Carrier and extraction solvent should be stated in your target spec.
Can the flavonoid content be verified before shipment?
COA and TDS can be arranged on request; testing scope is agreed per buyer specification. Tell us the marker, method and acceptance range you need, and we will confirm what documentation the partner producer can provide.
