Passionflower Extract

Passiflora incarnata — aerial-part extract standardized to vitexin and flavonoids, positioned around sleep and calm for supplement formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Passionflower is a well-established calming botanical traded widely in sleep, relaxation and evening-routine supplement categories. Extracts are typically standardized to vitexin or total flavonoids, with 2-4% vitexin commonly requested. We source through vetted partner producers and confirm each buyer's target marker, ratio and carrier before quotation.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is the extract standardized to vitexin or total flavonoids?
Both approaches are commonly traded. Vitexin at 2-4% is a frequent specification, and total flavonoid standardization is also requested. Tell us which marker you need and we will confirm feasibility.
Can a COA be arranged?
COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification.
Do you supply ratio extracts?
Yes, ratio extracts such as 4:1 and 10:1 are commonly requested in the market. Advise your target ratio and we will respond honestly on supply.
