FAQ

Common questions.

Is the extract standardized on thymoquinone, and how is it tested?
Thymoquinone is the usual marker and is commonly determined by HPLC, sometimes alongside a total volatile oil figure. Thymoquinone is volatile and can shift with storage and handling, so buyers often ask about the reporting basis and sample condition; the method is agreed per buyer specification.
What grades are commonly requested, and how do buyers check for dilution?
Commonly requested options in the market range from about 0.5% up to 5% thymoquinone, with extract-ratio and fixed-oil-bearing grades also traded. Adulteration checkpoints buyers raise include carrier dilution, addition of isolated thymoquinone and undeclared fixed oil content; the specific checks are agreed with your specification.
Can you supply documentation for my QA review?
A COA/TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. Tell us your target thymoquinone level, seed origin preference and required panel, and we will confirm feasibility honestly through our vetted partner producers.
