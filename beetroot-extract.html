Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Beetroot Extract is derived from the root of Beta vulgaris and is commonly standardized around natural nitrates and betaines, markers at the center of nitric-oxide-oriented sports nutrition lines. Demand is stable across powders, shots and capsules. FAQ

Common questions.

How is beetroot extract typically specified?
Buyers commonly request standardization around nitrate content or betaine levels. Share your target and we will confirm what can be sourced.
Is the extract available in powder and juice powder form?
Different powder formats are discussed in the market. Provide your target specification and we will confirm feasibility through partner producers.
Which beetroot extract documents come with a quotation?
For beetroot extract, a COA covering betalain or nitrate-related assay, heavy metals, microbial limits and identity is issued with each batch, alongside a TDS. These documents come from the producing partner and are checked against your specification before quotation, so confirm the marker and test scope you need.
How should beetroot extract be stored, and how do I pick between nitrate-standardized and betaine-standardized grades?
Beetroot extract is hygroscopic and its natural nitrate and betaine content can vary by lot, so store it sealed and away from moisture, heat and light and build a target range rather than a single figure into your spec. Nitrate standardization suits nitric-oxide-oriented sports positioning while betaine levels are often referenced in the same lines, so tell us which marker matters and your acceptable range, and we will confirm what can be sourced, with COA/TDS arranged on request.
