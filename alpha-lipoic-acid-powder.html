Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Alpha-lipoic acid is a sulfur-containing compound supplied as a yellow crystalline powder, standardised on assay content. Overseas supplement brands request it mainly for antioxidant-positioned capsules and blends, and demand has held steady as formulators pair it with vitamins and botanical antioxidants. As a Xi'an-based trading company we source ALA through vetted partner producers and confirm feasibility against each buyer's target specification before quoting.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is the material R-ALA, S-ALA or the racemic form, and how is assay verified?
Most market enquiries are for racemic alpha-lipoic acid at 99%+ by HPLC, though R-ALA-only grades are also traded at lower assay. We ask buyers to state which form and assay they need, then confirm with the partner producer whether that grade can be met. Test method and acceptance criteria are agreed per buyer specification, with COA and TDS arranged on request.
What should we check for when qualifying an alpha-lipoic acid lot?
Buyers commonly look at assay by HPLC, appearance and colour, moisture, residue on ignition, heavy metals and residual solvents, plus polymer content where a low-polymer grade is required. Because ALA is prone to polymerisation and discolouration on storage, we recommend agreeing packaging, storage and retest conditions up front. The exact testing scope is settled with the buyer and confirmed with the producer.
Can you supply against our own specification and documentation requirements?
Yes. Share your target assay, particle size, packaging format and required documents, and we will confirm feasibility honestly with our vetted partner producers — including where a request cannot be met as stated. COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification.
