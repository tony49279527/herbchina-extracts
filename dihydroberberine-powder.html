Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Dihydroberberine is a hydrogenated derivative of berberine, supplied as a high-purity powder for metabolic and glucose-metabolism product concepts. It has grown as an inquiry from brands building berberine-adjacent supplement lines with a different handling profile. FAQ

Common questions.

How is dihydroberberine made and how does it differ from berberine?
Dihydroberberine is a hydrogenated derivative of berberine, and buyers often ask about the conversion route and residual berberine content. These details are specified per buyer and confirmed with our partner producers before quoting.
Which assay grades are commonly requested?
The market most commonly asks for 98%+ by HPLC, with related-substance and residual-solvent limits varying between buyers. Tell us your target spec and we will confirm honestly what can be met.
What documentation is available?
COA and TDS can be arranged on request, with testing scope agreed per buyer specification. We do not assume a fixed documentation package because requirements differ by market.
