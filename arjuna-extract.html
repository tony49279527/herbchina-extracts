Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Buyers frequently request either tannin-standardized grades or arjunolic acid specifications, depending on whether the formulation leans on polyphenol content or a triterpenoid marker. FAQ

Common questions.

Should I specify tannins or arjunolic acid as the marker?
It depends on how the formulation is positioned. Tannin-based specifications are widely traded and are usually measured by a colorimetric or titration method, while arjunolic acid is a triterpenoid marker typically analyzed by HPLC. Some buyers set both. Let us know which marker your label or internal spec calls for so we can check what partner producers can support.
Which part of Terminalia arjuna is used, and does bark grade matter?
Commercial arjuna extract is normally made from the bark, and bark quality, age and collection practices influence the tannin and triterpenoid profile. Some supply chains include other plant parts, which can shift the analytical result. If your specification is bark-only, state that explicitly at inquiry stage.
What is the process for confirming a custom arjuna specification?
Send your target spec, including marker, method and any limits, and we will review feasibility with partner producers. COA and TDS are available on request, with testing scope confirmed against your target spec.
