Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Magnesium threonate is a magnesium salt of threonic acid, typically supplied as a white, water-soluble powder and commonly requested at high purity grades. It is used by formulators developing cognitive and sleep-positioned supplement lines. FAQ

Common questions.

How is magnesium threonate typically assayed, and what purity is commonly requested?
Assay is typically determined by titration or by HPLC, and magnesium content may be reported separately from threonate content. Buyers commonly request high-purity grades around 99%, but this is a reference market figure only and the actual specification is confirmed on inquiry against your method.
What does magnesium threonate powder typically look like, and how is it handled?
It is generally a white to off-white crystalline or powdered solid that is water-soluble, which suits beverage and capsule formats. Hygroscopic behaviour can vary between lots, so moisture and packaging requirements should be included in your target spec for our sourcing review.
Can you provide COA and TDS for magnesium threonate against our spec?
COA and TDS are available on request, with the testing scope confirmed against your target specification. As a trading company, we review your parameters and confirm feasibility with partner producers before documentation is provided.
