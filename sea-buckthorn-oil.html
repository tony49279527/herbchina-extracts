FAQ

Common questions.

Do buyers specify omega-7 content for sea buckthorn oil?
Yes, palmitoleic acid (omega-7) content is a common request, sometimes alongside omega-3, 6 and 9 profiles. These are commonly requested spec grades only; actual specs are confirmed on inquiry.
How does CO2 extraction differ from cold-pressed sea buckthorn oil?
CO2-extracted grades are often requested for fuller carotenoid and tocopherol retention, while cold-pressed routes are also common. Confirm your preferred route and we verify feasibility with partner producers against your target spec.
Can you supply COA and TDS for a specific sea buckthorn target?
COA and TDS are available on request, with the testing scope confirmed against your buyer's target spec. Send your required specification to begin.
