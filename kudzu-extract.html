FAQ

Common questions.

What is the difference between puerarin and total-isoflavone grades in kudzu extract?
Puerarin is a single isoflavone marker typically quantified by HPLC, while total isoflavones cover the broader group. The two are not interchangeable, so buyers should specify which basis their formulation requires; COA and TDS are available on request.
Which part of Pueraria lobata is used, and how do you check for root substitution?
Material is sourced from Pueraria lobata root. Buyers commonly review the HPLC profile of puerarin and related isoflavones to confirm identity and detect substitution with other Pueraria species. We share the testing scope we can arrange once your target spec is clear.
Can you confirm a kudzu extract grade at 40% or 98% puerarin for our formulation?
Send your target puerarin or total-isoflavone level and application, and we will confirm feasibility through our partner producers. Grades are commonly requested market specifications, not inventory commitments; documentation is provided on request.
