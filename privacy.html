Privacy Policy.

What we collect when you contact us, why we collect it, and how to ask us to delete it.

Last updated: September 25, 2026.

1. Who we are

HerbChina Extracts is a botanical and mushroom extract supplier based in Xi'an, China, founded in 2019. This policy explains how we handle information you share with us through this website.

2. Information we collect

When you submit an inquiry through our contact form, we collect the details you provide: your name, email address, company, country, the product you are interested in, and your message (such as target specifications or volumes). We also collect basic, anonymized website usage statistics through Google Analytics to understand which pages buyers find useful.

3. How we use it

We use your inquiry details solely to respond to your request — preparing quotations, confirming specifications, and communicating about potential orders. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes, and we do not add you to any mailing list without your explicit consent.

4. Data retention and deletion

We keep inquiry records only as long as needed to handle your request and any resulting business correspondence. You may ask us at any time to correct or delete your personal information by contacting us at +86 135 7292 7148 (phone / WeChat). We will act on deletion requests promptly.

5. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4), which sets cookies to measure aggregate site usage. You can block cookies in your browser settings; the site will continue to work, but some preferences may not be remembered.

6. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and publish the revised version on this page.
