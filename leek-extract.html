Leek Extract

Leek — savory allium botanical for seasoning and savory food formulations.

Overview

What it is, and how we handle it.

Leek extract is a botanical powder derived from the leaf and stem of Allium ampeloprasum, standardized around allicin and total-flavonoid content for savory applications. Demand has grown alongside clean-label seasoning, soup base and snack seasoning development. As a Xi'an-based trading company, we source through partner producers and confirm feasibility only after receiving your target specification.

FAQ

Common questions.

Which plant part is used for your leek extract, and how is allicin content tested?
Material is sourced from the leaf and stem of Allium ampeloprasum. Allicin is typically assessed by HPLC, though the exact marker and test method are agreed against your target spec before any offer is confirmed.
How do buyers verify leek extract and check for dilution with lower-cost allium material?
Buyers commonly cross-check allicin or total-flavonoid content against the declared grade and review organoleptic and TLC or HPLC profiles for consistency. We share the testing scope we can arrange once your target spec is clear, and COA/TDS are available on request.
Can you quote a leek extract grade against our own allicin or flavonoid specification?
Yes. Send your target marker, assay range and application, and we will confirm feasibility through our partner producers. Specifications are commonly requested grades, not inventory commitments; COA and TDS are available on request.
